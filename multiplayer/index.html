Rooms

Race 2 to 8 friends on the same boards, each on their own phone or computer, at the same time.

  1. Create a roomThe host gets a 6-character code and picks the difficulty, the world, the lives and how many games to play, up to 20.
  2. Invite friendsShare the code, the QR code or the invite link. On Android the link opens the app; on a computer or an iPhone it opens the browser game, already in the room.
  3. RaceAfter a 3-2-1 everyone gets the same board. Finishing first scores the most, and the hearts you keep and your time count too.
  4. PodiumAfter the last game a podium shows the winners. Play again keeps everyone together, and the match goes into each player's history.

Under the hood

Fair on a slow connection
Each board is built from the room code, so nothing on it has to travel over the network, and every player's own clock times their run.
One link, every device
Android App Links open the game straight from the invite; everyone else lands in the browser game, in the room.
The room carries on
If the host leaves or goes quiet, the player who joined first takes over.
Made to share
Every match is kept in your history, with a results picture sized to show whole on Instagram, WhatsApp and Viber.

Leaderboards

Every level has its own board, ranked by the fewest hearts lost and then the fastest time. Only clean runs count: no continues and no powerups.

    Profiles

    Pick a name and one of 12 avatars the first time you open a leaderboard. Tap anyone's row to see their stats and the maps they've published.

    • Names that stay put3 to 16 letters, digits or _, unique, and yours for good once saved.
    • Your own pictureOn Android, players signed in with Play Games can use a photo. Each one is re-encoded and checked by Google Cloud Vision before anyone sees it.
    • Your data, your call"Delete my leaderboard data" removes your name, picture, results and stats.

    Moderation

    Anything players make, from names and pictures to whole levels, can be reported. The rules are short, and they're public.

    Names
    A filter refuses rude words and reserved names, even when they're spelled with digits or underscores.
    Pictures
    Checked before anyone sees them, and players whose pictures keep being taken down can't upload any more.
    Reports
    Report a name, a picture or a map. Reporting a player also blocks them on your device at once.
    Three reports
    A picture comes down, a name is removed and its owner picks a new one, and a map is hidden until we've looked at it.
    Custom maps
    Creators accept the map rules first, the game checks every map can be cleared, and the creator clears it before it goes public.
    Just for you
    Block a player or hide a creator's maps. Nobody else is told.

    Custom maps

    Players build levels in the game's own editor: 5 to 40 cells each way, any of the 49 board shapes and 17 world looks, and every block the levels use. A check bar spots stuck arrows at once and rates the map from Easy to Expert.

    Published maps get a share code, a rating, a clear rate and a leaderboard of their own, and the Creators board ranks whose maps are played the most.
