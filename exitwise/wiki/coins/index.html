Coins

From the first level

Earned every level; spent on hints, continues and powerups.

How it works

  1. 1

    Spend them on hints (30), a continue with 3 hearts (100), and powerup packs in the shop.

  2. 2

    Coin packs are also for sale in the app's shop.

What it does

You start with 100 coins. Every level pays 10, a hard level 30, and a perfect clear (no mistakes) 5 more. The daily challenge pays 20, plus 5 for perfect, and your first clear of another player's custom map pays 10 to 30.
