Level-3 rock

161Autumn woodsArrives on level 161 · Autumn woods

The toughest rock: three bomb hits.

Arrives
Level 161 · Autumn woods

How to beat it

  1. 1

    Find all three bombs before you start: some are buried deeper than others.

  2. 2

    The arrows behind the rock can only go once the last bomb has hit.

What it does

A level-3 rock has three white dots and needs three bombs. Each hit cracks it and takes a dot away; the third breaks it.

Its bombs come from different arrows, usually from different sides of the rock.

Good to know

  • Level 161 introduces them with two level-3 rocks and nothing else. From then on about 8% of rocks are level 3, rising to 18% by level 300.
