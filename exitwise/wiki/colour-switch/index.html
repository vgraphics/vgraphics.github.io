Colour switch

Opens every gate of its colour at once, but not two-colour gates.

First appears on level 311

What it does

A colour switch is a dark square base with a round button in its colour, and that colour's symbol. It never blocks.

The first arrow, or bomb on its way to its rock, that passes over it presses it: every one-colour gate of that colour on the board opens, wherever it is, and the switch is used up. Two-colour gates don't react to switches.

How to beat it

  • A gate far away with no arrow of its colour in sight? Look for a switch of its colour on a free arrow's path.
  • A switch earlier on an arrow's own path opens a gate further along that same path before the arrow gets there.

Good to know

  • Level 311 introduces switches with one switch and its gate.
  • In-game tip: "A switch opens every gate of its colour."
