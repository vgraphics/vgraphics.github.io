Powerups

Auto-play

Plays a free arrow for you.

First appears on level 5

What it does

Auto-play picks an arrow that can leave right now and plays it for you, so it never costs a heart. That can be a bomb whose path to its rock is clear. It never picks an arrow hidden under a shutter.

How to use it

  • Use it when you're stuck and don't want to spend a heart finding the way.
  • A pack of 3 costs 100 coins in the shop. It's also a daily-challenge milestone reward.

Good to know

  • A run that uses a powerup doesn't go on the leaderboard.
