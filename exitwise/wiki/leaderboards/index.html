Basics

Leaderboards & profiles

Every level has a board: fewest hearts lost, then fastest time.

What it does

Each level has its own leaderboard, ranked by fewest hearts lost, then by the fastest time. You see the top 10 and your own row.

Only clean runs count: no continue and no powerup. Hints are fine. Your best run stays on the board.

How it works for you

  • The first time you open a leaderboard you can pick a username (3 to 16 letters, digits or _) and one of 12 avatars. Without one you show as "Player" and a short code.
  • Settings → Profile → "Delete my leaderboard data" removes your name and results.
