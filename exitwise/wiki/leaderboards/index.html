Leaderboards & profiles

Basics

A board for every level, plus four global boards.

How it works

  1. 1

    You start with an automatic username from VHub, two words and three digits. In your profile (tap your account card in Settings) you can change it once (3 to 16 letters, digits or _) and pick one of 12 avatars; the name you choose is yours for good.

  2. 2

    Tap any row to see that player's profile: their stats and published maps, and Add friend to send them a VHub friend request (Request sent, with Cancel, until they answer; Accept when they asked you first). Friends are the same in every VGraphics™ game. On a profile, sign in first; as a guest you can still add friends by friend code on VHub.

  3. 3

    Your name, avatar and picture are your VHub profile, the same in every VGraphics game. To use a photo of your own in place of the avatar, choose it on VHub (in Settings, tap your account card to open your profile, then "Choose a photo in VHub"); every photo is checked before anyone sees it.

  4. 4

    Levelling up on VHub unlocks rank frames, from wood at level 5 to mythic at level 275. Choose yours on your VHub profile: it shows around your picture on the leaderboards, your profile, in rooms and in match history.

  5. 5

    In your profile (tap your account card in Settings), "Delete my Exitwise™ data" takes you off the boards and removes your results, stats and published maps, in Exitwise and on VHub; your name and picture stay, since they're your VGraphics account's. "Delete my VGraphics account", in the small print at the foot of Settings, removes them too, in every VGraphics game.

What it does

Each level has its own leaderboard, ranked by fewest hearts lost, then by the fastest time. You see the top 10 and your own row.

Only clean runs count: no continue and no powerup.

Hints and Prince Arlo's skills are fine. Your best run stays on the board.

The Global tab ranks everyone on four boards: Score (points for every level won), Perfect (levels won without losing a heart), Maps (other players' custom maps cleared) and Creators (plays of your own maps).

The top 100 of each are also on the website's global ranking page.

Your score is the total of the points of every level you've won, and the more arrows a level has, the more it is worth.

A win starts from 650 points plus 10 for each arrow on the board: level 100, with 104 arrows, is worth up to 1,690, and level 500, with 430, up to 4,950. Each level keeps the most points you've earned on it, so a replay can only raise your score. Only the levels on the map count, not the daily challenge, rooms or custom maps.

A win loses 25 points for each heart lost, 100 for each hint or powerup, 200 for each continue and some for the time, but it always earns at least 100.

Every hint counts, free or paid; Prince Arlo's skills, and a powerup a tutorial asks you to try, cost nothing. A continue costs the same with coins or a video. The time costs 220 points a second, divided by the board's arrows (a board under 10 arrows counts as 10): a minute on level 100 costs 126. So winning level 100 in a minute with one heart lost earns 1,690 − 25 − 126 = 1,539. Levels you won before this score came in count from the best run your game kept.
