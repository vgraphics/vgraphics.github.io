Leaderboards & profiles

Basics

A board for every level, plus four global boards.

How it works

  1. 1

    The first time you open a leaderboard you can pick a username (3 to 16 letters, digits or _) and one of 12 avatars. A name is yours for good once saved. Without one you show as "Player" and a short code.

  2. 2

    Tap any row to see that player's profile: their stats and published maps.

  3. 3

    On Android, players signed in with Play Games can use a picture of their own; every picture is checked before anyone sees it.

  4. 4

    Settings → Profile → "Delete my leaderboard data" removes your name, picture, results and stats.

What it does

Each level has its own leaderboard, ranked by fewest hearts lost, then by the fastest time. You see the top 10 and your own row.

Only clean runs count: no continue and no powerup.

Hints are fine. Your best run stays on the board.

The Global tab ranks everyone on four boards: Score (points for every level won), Perfect (levels won without losing a heart), Maps (other players' custom maps cleared) and Creators (plays of your own maps).

The top 100 of each are also on the website's global ranking page.

Your score: each level you win is worth 1,000 points, less 25 for each heart lost and less for the time taken (scaled by the size of the board), and never less than 100.

A win with a continue or a powerup is worth 100; hints don't cost points. Each level keeps the most points you have earned on it, so replaying a level can only raise your score.
