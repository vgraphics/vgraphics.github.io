Magic wand

65VolcanoArrives on level 65 · Volcano

Clears up to 5 free arrows at once.

Arrives
Level 65 · Volcano

How to use it

  1. 1

    Use it on big boards to thin out the easy arrows quickly, or to count down ice and shutters fast.

  2. 2

    A pack of 3 costs 300 coins. It's also the reward for 25 daily challenges in a month.

What it does

The magic wand clears up to 5 arrows that are free to leave, all at once: the ones nearest the middle of the screen. It never costs a heart.

A bomb with a clear path to its rock counts as free and hits the rock.

An arrow hidden under a shutter is never free, so the wand never takes one. Arrows the wand frees stay on the board for your next move.

Good to know

  • The shop also sells a bundle with one of each powerup for 200 coins.
