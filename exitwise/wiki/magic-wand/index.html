Magic wand

Clears up to 5 free arrows at once.

First appears on level 65

What it does

The magic wand clears up to 5 arrows that are free to leave, all at once.

How to use it

  • Use it on big boards to thin out the easy arrows quickly.
  • A pack of 3 costs 300 coins. It's also the reward for 25 daily challenges in a month.

Good to know

  • The shop also sells a bundle with one of each powerup for 200 coins.
