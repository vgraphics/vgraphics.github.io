Obstacles

Things on the board that change where your arrows can go, and the arrows that deal with them. A new one arrives every 20 to 30 levels, starts on an easy level built to teach it, and then stays, mixed in with the ones before it.

21Jungle RockStops every arrow until its bombs blow it up. 21Jungle Bomb arrowFlies to its rock and blows it up, once its path is clear. 41Volcano Colour gateLets only its own colour through, then opens for good. 61Volcano MirrorTurns an arrow's path by 90 degrees. 81Desert Level-2 rockA tougher rock that takes two bomb hits. 101Lagoon PortalCarries an arrow's path across the board to its pair. 131Lagoon IceA block with a number that melts as arrows leave. 161Autumn woods Level-3 rockThe toughest rock: three bomb hits. 191Autumn woods Paint tileGives an arrow that passes over it its colour. 221Crystal caves ShutterA roller door that hides arrows until it rolls up. 251Crystal caves Two-colour gateA gate that needs two colours, in order. 281Clockwork town Chain bomb & rock wallsOne blast runs along a whole wall of touching rocks. 311Clockwork town Keys and doorsA key unlocks every door of its colour at once, but not two-colour gates.

Powerups

One-tap helpers. You get 3 of each for free when it unlocks; more cost coins in the shop. None of them ever costs a heart, and none touches an arrow hidden under a shutter.

5Meadow Auto-playPlays a free arrow for you. 25Jungle Grid linesShows the path of every arrow you can see, for 5 seconds. 45Volcano Remove arrowRemoves any arrow you can see, even a blocked one, without costing a heart. 65Volcano Magic wandClears up to 5 free arrows at once.

Basics

How the game works: hearts, coins, hints, hard levels, worlds, and the ways to play with others.

How to playTap an arrow with a clear path. Clear the board. HeartsYou have 3. Tapping a blocked arrow costs one. CoinsEarned every level; spent on hints, continues and powerups. HintsLights up an arrow that can leave right now. 10Meadow Hard levelsEvery 10th level: bigger, deeper, at night, for 30 coins. 5Meadow Board shapes49 shapes, from hexagons and spirals to rockets and castles. Worlds & the level map17 worlds, each with its own look, on one scrolling map. Daily challengeOne board a day, the same for everyone, with monthly rewards. Leaderboards & profilesA board for every level, plus four global boards. Play with friendsRace 2 to 8 players on the same boards with a room code. Custom mapsBuild your own levels, and play other players' maps.
