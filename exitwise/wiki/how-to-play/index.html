Basics

How to play

Tap an arrow with a clear path. Clear the board.

First appears on level 1

What it does

Every arrow points somewhere. Tap one and it slides off the board along its path, but only if nothing is in its way.

Clear every arrow to win the level. Each arrow you clear can free others.

How it works for you

  • Start with arrows whose path to the edge is already empty.
  • On big boards, pinch or scroll to zoom and drag to move.
