Powerups

Remove arrow

Removes any arrow, even a blocked one, without costing a heart.

First appears on level 45

What it does

Tap Remove arrow, then tap any arrow: it disappears, even if it was blocked, and you don't lose a heart.

How to use it

  • Save it for the one arrow that blocks everything else.
  • A pack of 3 costs 250 coins.

Good to know

  • A run that uses a powerup doesn't go on the leaderboard.

Works with
