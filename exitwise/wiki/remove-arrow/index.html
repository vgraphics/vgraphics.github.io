Powerups

Remove arrow

Removes any arrow you can see, even a blocked one, without costing a heart.

First appears on level 45

What it does

Tap Remove arrow, then tap any arrow you can see: it disappears, even if it was blocked, and you don't lose a heart.

Whatever that arrow would have done on its way out still happens, all along its path, even past whatever blocked it: a bomb hits the first rock on its path, gates of its colour open (the colour it has there, after any paint tile), switches it passes are pressed, a two-colour gate uses up its pip, and every ice block and shutter counts down by one.

How to use it

  • Save it for the one arrow that blocks everything else.
  • A pack of 3 costs 250 coins.

Good to know

  • It never takes an arrow hidden under a shutter, even partly hidden, because you can't see it. Tapping the shutter only rattles it and tells you hidden arrows can't be removed: nothing is used, and Remove arrow stays ready for an arrow you can see.
  • A run that uses a powerup doesn't go on the leaderboard.
