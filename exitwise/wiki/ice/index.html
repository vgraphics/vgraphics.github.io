Ice

131LagoonArrives on level 131 · Lagoon

A block with a number that melts as arrows leave.

Arrives
Level 131 · Lagoon

How to beat it

  1. 1

    Arrows pointing at the ice have to wait: clear the other arrows first.

  2. 2

    The number is just big enough: the ice melts right as you run out of other arrows to clear, so spend your moves on the arrows that don't wait on it.

  3. 3

    With more than one ice block or shutter on a board, their numbers are always well apart: at least a twentieth of the board's arrows, and never under 3. Ice and ink a guardian casts mid-fight, and frost a rival sends in a room, keep at least 3 from every number already on the board. Each one opens at its own moment, so plan which arrows to clear before the next one goes.

What it does

Ice is one cell showing a number. It holds no arrow and stops every arrow, bombs too, and a bomb can't break it.

Each time any arrow leaves the board, by a tap or a powerup, the number drops by one.

At 0 the ice melts and the way is open.

In-game tip

Ice melts as arrows leave.

Got it

Good to know

  • Level 131 introduces ice with one block counting from 5, and a tutorial that shows an arrow waiting on it.
