Obstacles

Ice

A block with a number that melts as arrows leave.

First appears on level 131

What it does

Ice is one cell showing a number. It holds no arrow and stops every arrow, bombs too, and a bomb can't break it.

Each time any arrow leaves the board, by a tap or a powerup, the number drops by one. At 0 the ice melts and the way is open.

How to beat it

  • Arrows pointing at the ice have to wait: clear the other arrows first.
  • The number is just big enough: the ice melts right as you run out of other arrows to clear, so spend your moves on the arrows that don't wait on it.

Good to know

  • Level 131 introduces ice with one block counting from 5, and a tutorial that shows an arrow waiting on it.
  • In-game tip: "Ice melts as arrows leave."
