Arrives
Level 5 · Meadow

How it works

  1. 1

    Holes, notches and narrow parts change which edge an arrow can reach: check the path, not just the direction.

  2. 2

    Cells outside the shape are empty space that arrows fly over.

What it does

Boards come in 49 shapes. 18 plain shapes stretch to fit the phone: the rectangle, rounded, circle, diamond, cross, notches, holes, ring, hourglass, arch, blob, hexagon, triangle, ell, zigzag, stairs, chevron and spiral. 31 pictures keep their own proportions: heart, star, tree, house, cat, fish, mushroom, cup, butterfly, flower, leaf, flame, gem, lightning, moon, cactus, pyramid, rocket, anchor, boat, crown, key, bell, ghost, snowflake, gear, cloud, candy, acorn, turtle and castle.

Levels 1 to 4 are rectangles; after that only a few are.

New shapes unlock a few at a time up to level 100, and many worlds have pictures of their own, like the flame and gem in Volcano or the snowflake in Glacier. The longest board side grows up to 100 cells.

From level 25 about 1 in 6 normal levels is a knot level, where arrows zig-zag at nearly every cell.
