Basics

Board shapes

20 shapes, from rectangles to hearts, cats and butterflies.

First appears on level 5

What it does

Boards come in 20 shapes: rectangle, rounded, circle, diamond, heart, cross, ring, holes, notches, hourglass, star, blob, tree, house, cat, fish, mushroom, cup, butterfly and arch. Levels 1 to 4 are always rectangles.

From level 25 about 1 in 6 levels is a zig-zag level, where arrows twist into knots. The longest board side grows up to 100 cells.

How it works for you

  • Holes and notches change which edge an arrow can reach: check the path, not just the direction.
