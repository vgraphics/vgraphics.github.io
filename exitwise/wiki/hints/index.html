Basics

Hints

Lights up an arrow that can leave right now.

First appears on level 1

What it does

A hint highlights an arrow whose path is clear. You get 3 free hints; after that a hint costs 30 coins, or, on Android, a short video.

How it works for you

  • Hints are allowed on leaderboard runs, unlike powerups and continues.
