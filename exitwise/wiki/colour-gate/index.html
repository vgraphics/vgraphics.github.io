Colour gate

Lets only its own colour through, then opens for good.

First appears on level 41

What it does

A gate is one cell in blue or yellow (purple from level 101), drawn as a coloured frame with a symbol: a circle, a triangle or a diamond, so you can match it without relying on colour.

An arrow of the gate's colour passes through and the gate opens behind it for good; after that every arrow gets through. Arrows of other colours, and bombs, bump into a closed gate.

How to beat it

  • Find the arrow with the gate's colour and symbol, and send it through first.
  • Later obstacles open gates too: a paint tile can give an arrow the gate's colour, and a key of its colour unlocks it at once (on a board with a key, its gates are drawn as locked doors).

Good to know

  • Gates first appear on level 41, with one gate and a free arrow of its colour heading through it.
  • In-game tip: "Only a gate's own colour gets through."
