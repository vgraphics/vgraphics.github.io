Basics

Worlds & the level map

17 worlds, each with its own look, on one scrolling map.

What it does

The levels are split into 17 worlds: Meadow (1–20), Jungle (21–40), Volcano (41–80), Desert (81–100), Lagoon (101–140), Mushroom forest (141–160), Autumn woods (161–200), Glacier (201–220), Crystal caves (221–260), Cherry blossom (261–280), Clockwork town (281–320), Coral reef (321–340), Bayou (341–380), Candy land (381–400), Temple ruins (401–440), Sky islands (441–460) and Starlight (461–500). After level 500 they come round again from Jungle.

Each world has its own scenery, board colours, arrow style and the puff an arrow leaves as it takes off. Hard levels are the same world at night.

How it works for you

  • The map is the home screen: scroll it, tap a finished level to play it again, or tap Play for the next one.
  • Replays don't pay coins. Winning a world's last level opens the next world.
