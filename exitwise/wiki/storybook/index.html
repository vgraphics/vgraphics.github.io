How it works

  1. 1

    The prologue plays the first time you open the map. After that, a world's pages play over the map when you reach it and when you free its guardian, never during a level. Tap or swipe for the next page, or skip them all at any time.

  2. 2

    Tap Story on the map to read any chapter you have reached again; a dot on the button means pages you haven't read yet. Chapters further up stay locked, with a line that tells you which world to free first. A freed guardian puts its medal on its world's chapter.

  3. 3

    Turn off "Show story pages" in Settings if you'd rather not see them by themselves: the book still fills up, and you can read it from the Story button whenever you like.

  4. 4

    On the web, the same book is on vgraphics.gr/exitwise/storybook/. It opens the chapters you have reached in Exitwise in your browser, in the same browser.

What it does

Exitwise has a story, told in 81 paintings with a line each: The Tangled Tower. The Duskweaver has knotted the Dawn Door at the top of the Tower of Worlds and tangled every world with his arrows. Prince Arlo, a young knight archer, and Pip the owl climb the tower world by world to break the curse.

The book has a prologue, a chapter for each of the 17 worlds, and a finale with its epilogue.

A world's chapter has two parts: its opening, when you reach the world, and its ending, when you free the world's guardian on its last level.

Each page plays like a little film: the painting fills the screen and the camera drifts slowly over it while its line fades in, with the story's own music.

Upright screens get a tall painting of their own, and big screens a sharper copy.

Good to know

  • The book's seven songs, from the prologue's music box to the Dawn Door, are on the soundtrack.
  • The finale and the epilogue open at level 500.
