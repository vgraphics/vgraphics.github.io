Rock

21JungleArrives on level 21 · Jungle

Stops every arrow until its bombs blow it up.

Arrives
Level 21 · Jungle

How it works

Tap an arrow with a clear path.
  1. 1

    Find the bomb whose path leads to the rock.

  2. 2

    Clear the arrows in the bomb's way first, then tap the bomb.

  3. 3

    Until then, plan around the rock: any arrow pointing at it bumps and costs a heart.

What it does

A rock is 1 to 4 connected cells that hold no arrow and stop every arrow that runs into it. It looks the same in every world: a warm stone with a dark outline. It stays until the bomb arrows aimed at it have hit it.

Arrows may point straight at a rock: they're blocked until it's gone.

Good to know

  • Rocks first appear on level 21, where a short tutorial walks you through clearing a bomb's path and firing it.
  • Tougher rocks come later: level-2 rocks from level 81, level-3 rocks from 161, and whole rock walls from 281.
