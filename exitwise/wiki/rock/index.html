Traps & obstacles

Rock

Stops every arrow. Only a bomb can clear it.

First appears on level 21

What it does

A rock holds no arrow and stops every arrow that runs into it. It stays there until a bomb arrow aimed at it blows it up.

How to use it

  • Look for the bomb arrow whose path leads to the rock.
  • Free the bomb's path first, then tap the bomb.
  • Until the rock is gone, plan around it: any arrow pointing at it is blocked.

Good to know

  • Rocks first appear on level 21, two single rocks. They get more common until level 70.
