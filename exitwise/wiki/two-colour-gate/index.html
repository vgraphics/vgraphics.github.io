Two-colour gate

A gate that needs two colours, in order.

First appears on level 251

What it does

A two-colour gate holds two symbols: the first on the left and bigger. Its frame shows the colour it lets through next.

At first only the first colour gets through, which uses up its pip. Then the second colour gets through too, and when it passes the gate opens for good. Other colours and bombs bump until then, and colour switches don't open it.

How to beat it

  • Send an arrow of the big symbol's colour first, then one of the small symbol's colour.
  • If Remove arrow takes an arrow of the second colour whose path runs through the gate before the first colour has passed, its pip is still used: the gate then opens for good as soon as the first colour passes.

Good to know

  • Level 251 introduces them with a single two-colour gate.
  • In-game tip: "Two-colour gate: big symbol goes first."
