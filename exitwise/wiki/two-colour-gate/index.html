Two-colour gate

A gate that needs two colours, in order.

First appears on level 251

What it does

A two-colour gate holds two symbols: the first on the left and bigger. Its frame shows the colour it lets through next.

At first only the first colour gets through, which uses up its pip. Then the second colour gets through too, and when it passes the gate opens for good. Other colours and bombs bump until then.

How to beat it

  • Send an arrow of the big symbol's colour first, then one of the small symbol's colour.
  • If a powerup removes an arrow of the second colour early, its pip is still used, so the gate never closes on you.

Good to know

  • Level 251 introduces them with a single two-colour gate.
  • In-game tip: "Two-colour gate: big symbol goes first."
