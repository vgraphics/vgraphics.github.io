Bomb arrow

Flies to its rock and blows it up, once its path is clear.

First appears on level 21

What it does

A bomb arrow is drawn in charcoal (pale grey on night boards) with a flickering orange spark at its tip. Tap it and it flies along its path to the first rock and hits it.

No bomb starts free: every bomb has a guard, an arrow standing on its path between the bomb and its rock. Clear the guard first, and whatever holds the guard up.

Bombs have no colour, so closed colour gates stop them, and so do arrows, ice and shutters. A blocked bomb bumps like any arrow and costs a heart.

How to beat it

  • Trace the bomb's path to its rock and find the arrow in the way.
  • Free that arrow, then tap the bomb.
  • Each bomb hits once: a level-2 or level-3 rock needs that many bombs, usually from different sides.

Good to know

  • The level-21 tutorial points out a rock ("New: rocks and bombs"), then the arrow in a bomb's way, then the bomb.
