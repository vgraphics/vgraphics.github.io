How it works

Tap an arrow with a clear path.
  1. 1

    Trace the bomb’s path to its rock and find the arrow in the way.

  2. 2

    Free that arrow, then tap the bomb.

  3. 3

    It flies along its path to the first rock and blows it up with a flash.

What it does

A bomb arrow looks like a bomb: a round charcoal head with a pointed nose that shows the way it flies, on a fuse with a flickering orange spark at the end. It looks the same on every board. Tap it and it flies along its path to the first rock and blows it up with a flash.

No bomb starts free: every bomb has a guard, an arrow standing on its path between the bomb and its rock.

Clear the guard first, and whatever holds the guard up.

Bombs have no colour, so closed colour gates stop them, and so do arrows, ice and shutters.

A blocked bomb bumps like any arrow and costs a heart.

Tip

Free the guard first

No bomb starts free: clear its guard first, and whatever holds the guard up.
