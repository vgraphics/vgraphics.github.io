Hard levels

Every 10th level: bigger, deeper, at night, for 30 coins.

Arrives
Level 10 · Meadow

How it works

  1. 1

    Zoom out first to see the whole board, then work from the edges in.

  2. 2

    A hard level pays 30 coins, plus 5 for a perfect clear.

What it does

Every 10th level, from level 10, is a hard level: about 1.45 times as big as the levels around it, always a picture shape, played at night on a dark board. It keeps the deepest of three boards, so it's always harder than the nine levels before it.

Hard levels get more of the newer obstacles too.

The two levels after a hard level are a little smaller, to catch your breath.
