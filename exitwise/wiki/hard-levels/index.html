Basics

Hard levels

Every 10th level: a bigger board, and 30 coins.

First appears on level 10

What it does

Every 10th level, from level 10, is a hard level: about half as big again as the levels around it, and the trickiest of several candidate boards.

From level 20, hard levels are always picture shapes. The two levels after a hard level are a little smaller, to catch your breath.

How it works for you

  • Zoom out first to see the whole board, then work from the edges in.
  • A hard level pays 30 coins, plus 5 for a perfect clear.
