The arrow puzzle you can play right here

Every arrow on the board points somewhere. Tap one and it slides away, but only if nothing stands in its way. Find the order that sets them all free, from a gentle first board to puzzles bigger than your screen.

How to play

  1. Look where each arrow points. Its path runs straight on to the edge of the board.

  2. Tap an arrow whose path is clear, and off it goes. Tap a blocked one and you lose one of your 3 hearts.

  3. Clear every arrow to win. Clearing one can only ever free others, so no level turns impossible halfway.

Stuck?

A hint lights up an arrow that can leave right now. The first 3 are free, then they cost 30 coins.

On a big board, drag to move around and pinch to see all of it.

Questions

Is Exitwise free?

Yes, here and on Android. You earn coins by playing: 10 for a level, 30 for a hard one, and 5 more for a perfect clear.

Do I need an account?

No, you can play straight away. Signing in with Google keeps your progress and your settings on every device and puts your name on the leaderboards.

Can every level be solved?

Yes. Every level comes from a fixed seed, so everyone gets the same board, and each one is tested to be solvable before it ships.
