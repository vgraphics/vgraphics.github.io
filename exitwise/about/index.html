About Exitwise

How to play

Every arrow on the board points somewhere. Give one a tap and it slides away, as long as nothing stands in its path. Find the order that sets them all free, from a gentle first board to puzzles bigger than your screen.

  1. Look where each arrow points. Its path runs straight on to the edge of the board.
  2. Pick an arrow with nothing in front of it, and off it goes. Pick a blocked one and one of your 3 hearts is gone.
  3. Free every arrow and the level is yours. Clearing one can only ever free others, so no level turns impossible halfway.
  • Stuck?A hint points out an arrow that is free to go this moment. The first 3 are free, then they cost 30 coins.
  • A big board?Drag to move around it, and pinch to see all of it.

Questions

Can every level be solved?

Yes. Each level is built from a fixed seed, which hands every player the same board, and each one is checked to be solvable before it ships.

What counts as my best on a level?

The win with the fewest hearts lost, and between two equal wins, the faster one. Each level keeps its own best, and the panel at the end of a level shows it beside your time.

Why do the levels after a hard one feel easier?

On purpose: after each hard level come two slightly smaller boards, a breather before the climb goes on.
