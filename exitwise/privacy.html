Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Exitwise (the "game") on Android, iOS or the web. The game is made by VGraphics ("we", "us").

The short version

  • You don't need to sign in. We never ask for your real name or contact details; if you choose to sign in with a Google account, Google tells our sign-in service your email address and name, and we use them only to sign you in.
  • Your progress and settings are saved on your device. If you sign in with Google Play Games or a Google account (Android), with Google on our website, or with your CrazyGames account (CrazyGames), they are also saved to that account so you can continue on another device.
  • Every level has a leaderboard. Your best time and hearts lost on each level are shown there to other players, with a username and picture you choose, or "Player" and a short code if you don't choose one. The global ranking (top 100 players by level, perfect clears and custom maps) is also public on our website.
  • On Android you can use your own photo as your picture. It is checked automatically before anyone else can see it, and other players can report pictures and names.
  • When you play with friends in a room, the others in the room see your username, preset picture and results. Rooms are deleted automatically a day after they were made.
  • If you publish a custom map, every player can see and play it, with your username as its creator. Reports about maps, and any message you add to them, are read only by us.
  • You can delete your cloud save, your username, your picture and your leaderboard results in the game at any time.
  • The free game shows ads. Our advertising partners may collect device information to show and measure ads.
  • We use Firebase (by Google) to see how the game is played and to receive crash reports. This data doesn't include your name or contact details.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your current level, your coins and hints, your best time on each level, your sound and vibration settings, and what you have bought (such as "Remove Ads"). This is kept in the app's local storage on your phone or tablet, or in your browser's storage on the web. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes it.

Your game account

So that leaderboards know which results are yours, the game gives every player an account with Google Firebase Authentication. It is a random identifier with no name or email attached. If you sign in with Google Play Games on Android, that account is linked to your Play Games player ID, and your Play Games name may be offered as a username suggestion. On the web (CrazyGames), the game keeps this identifier in your CrazyGames save data, so it follows your CrazyGames account to other browsers; CrazyGames handles its own login under the CrazyGames Privacy Policy, and we receive your CrazyGames username only to suggest it as your name.

You can also sign in with a Google account: on Android in Settings → "Link Google account", and on our website (vgraphics.gr) with "Sign in". This lets you play with the same progress on your phone and on the website. Google then shares your Google account's email address, name and profile picture link with Firebase Authentication, which keeps them with your game account. We use them only to sign you in and to show which Google account is linked; they are never shown to other players, shared or used for ads. If your Google account already belongs to another game account, the game joins the two into one: it asks which progress to keep, and deletes the other game account with its username and leaderboard results.

Cloud save

On Android, when you sign in with Google Play Games, and on our website when you sign in with Google, a copy of your saved game (the same information as on your device) is stored in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore under your game account, so you can continue on another device. If this device and your account both have progress, the game asks which one to keep. On CrazyGames, your save is kept by CrazyGames in your browser or, once you log in, in your CrazyGames account.

Leaderboards and your profile

When you win a level without a continue or a powerup, your result for that level (the hearts you lost and your time) is sent to the level's leaderboard in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore, together with your game account identifier. Only your best result per level is kept. If you are offline, the result waits on your device and is sent later.

Leaderboards are visible to everyone who plays the game. They show your username and picture if you set a profile, or "Player" and the first characters of your account identifier if you didn't. The first time you open a leaderboard, the game asks whether you want to choose a username (3 to 16 letters, digits or underscores) and one of the game's preset pictures, or on Android a photo of your own (see below). A username can't be changed once it's set, so choose carefully; your picture can be changed in Settings → Profile. Please don't use your real name or anything that identifies you. Names that are rude or pretend to be us are refused, and names that other players report are taken away (see "Reports and blocking").

The game also keeps your player stats under your game account, such as your highest level, perfect clears and the custom maps you have cleared, from your first win. The Global tab ranks players by them. The same global ranking is published on our website at vgraphics.gr/exitwise/ranking, where anyone can see it without playing or signing in: the top 100 players on each global board, with their username and picture (or "Player" and a short code) and the numbers that board counts. Nothing else about you is shown there. A name or picture taken away after reports isn't shown, and deleting your leaderboard data also takes you off the ranking.

Playing with friends (rooms)

To play with friends, the game needs your username (it asks you to choose one first). When you create or join a room, the room is stored in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore under its 6-character code: the room's settings, and for each player their game account identifier, username and preset picture, when they joined and were last active, and how each game ended for them (cleared, out of hearts or out of time, with the time and the hearts lost). Anyone who has the room's code can see this. Rooms are deleted automatically a day after they were made.

Inviting friends shows a QR code with the room's link and opens your phone's share sheet, or copies the invite. On the web the link leads to the game where you are playing it: on CrazyGames, where the game also tells CrazyGames which room you are in so that your CrazyGames friends can join you there, or on our website (vgraphics.gr). In the app it leads to a page on our website that opens the game in that room.

Profile pictures

On Android, if you sign in with Google Play Games, you can use a photo from your phone as your picture instead of a preset one. You choose it with Android's photo picker, so the game only ever sees the photo you pick. The game cuts it to a square and sends it to our server (Google Cloud Functions). The server makes a small copy (192 × 192 pixels) without the photo's hidden details, such as where and with which camera it was taken. It then checks that copy with Google Cloud Vision SafeSearch for nudity, violence and similar content, before anyone else can see it. Google processes the picture on our behalf only to check it. Pictures that pass are stored in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore and shown to every player next to your name. Pictures that don't pass aren't kept. You can remove your picture at any time in Settings → Profile.

Don't use pictures with nudity or sexual content, violence or gore, hate symbols, someone else's photo without their permission, or personal details such as a phone number or address. The game shows these rules before your first picture.

Custom maps

You can make your own maps in the game's map editor. Maps you haven't published yet (drafts) are saved on your device and, when you are online, under your game account in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore, so they follow your account to another device. Other players can't see your drafts.

To publish a map you need a username and must accept the map rules. A published map is stored in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore with its name, size, shape, arrows and obstacles, the world look you chose, its share code and difficulty, when it was published, and your game account identifier. Every player can see a published map, with your username and picture as its creator, and its totals: plays, clears and average rating. The clear you made before publishing becomes the first entry on the map's leaderboard. A published map can't be edited, but you can unpublish it, and then nobody else can see or play it.

When you play someone else's map, the game stores that you started it and whether you cleared it, and, if you rate it, your rating (1 to 5 stars), each with your game account identifier. The map's creator and other players never see these records, only the totals. Your best run on a map goes on that map's leaderboard, which works like the level leaderboards above. The number of other players' maps you have cleared, and for a creator the plays, ratings and stars their maps received, are also counted in your player stats under your game account, which other players can see.

Your saved game (on your device, and in your cloud save) also keeps the maps you have cleared and been paid coins for, your best times on maps, the maps you reported, the creators whose maps you hid (their game account identifiers and names), and which version of the map rules you accepted.

Reports and blocking

Tap another player on a leaderboard to report their picture or name, or to block them. Blocking hides their picture and name on your device only. A report hides them for you too, and is stored with your game account identifier and the reported player's. When three different players who are signed in (with Google Play Games or Google, or with CrazyGames) report the same picture, it is removed automatically. A player who loses two pictures this way can't upload pictures any more. When three report the same username, it is taken away and stays reserved so that nobody can use it again, and its owner chooses a new one. So that a removed picture or name can't come straight back, we keep these moderation records even after you delete your data: the counts, the game account identifiers of the reporters and of the uploader (linked to their Google Play Games player ID, Google account or CrazyGames user ID), and a username that is being reported or was removed.

You can also report a custom map, from its page or after playing it. You choose a reason (an offensive drawing or shape, an offensive name, broken, spam or a copy, or something else) and can add a message of up to 500 characters. The report is stored in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore with the map, your reason and message, the time, and your game account identifier. Only we read map reports; the map's creator and other players can't. The map disappears for you at once. When three different players report the same map, it is hidden from everyone until we have reviewed it, and we then keep, hide or remove it. We can also stop a creator from publishing, which hides all their maps; we keep their game account identifier on a list of banned creators for as long as the ban lasts. Map reports and that list are moderation records too, and we keep them even after you delete your data.

On a map's page you can also hide every map by its creator ("Hide name's maps"). This only changes what you see, and the creator isn't told. You can show their maps again in Settings → Hidden creators.

Deleting your data

  • Username, picture and leaderboard results: Settings → Profile → "Delete my leaderboard data" deletes your profile and picture, frees your username and removes all your results from every leaderboard. You can then choose a new username.
  • Cloud save and account (Android, and our website): Settings → "Delete cloud save" deletes your cloud save, your profile, picture and leaderboard results, and your game account with the Google account details linked to it. It also deletes your map drafts, your plays and ratings of maps and your player stats, and takes all your published maps down, so nobody can see or play them any more. The progress on your device or in your browser stays.
  • One published map: open it in the Mine tab of Custom maps and unpublish it.
  • On CrazyGames: your save is managed by CrazyGames (see their privacy policy).
  • You can also email us at the address below and we will delete it for you.

Data you don't delete is kept for as long as the game runs its leaderboards, custom maps and cloud saves.

Analytics and crash reports

On Android and iOS we use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. Analytics tells us things like which levels players finish and where they give up, so we can tune the difficulty. Crashlytics sends a report when the game crashes, so we can fix it. They collect an app instance identifier, your device model, operating system and language, your approximate country, and in-game events or crash details. We use this only to improve the game, never to identify you or to show you ads. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

On the web we use Google Analytics for the same purpose: the same game events, and error reports when something goes wrong in the game. It doesn't receive your name, email or Google account, and it uses an identifier stored in your browser. On our website it only runs if you allow it when the game first opens (if Google's consent message there has already asked you about analytics, we use that answer instead of asking again); you can change your answer with the "Statistics" link under the game.

Advertising

To keep the game free, it shows ads: a small banner, occasional ads between levels, and optional video ads you can choose to watch for a reward, such as continuing a level. The ads are delivered by these partners:

  • On Android and iOS: Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies) and the ad networks it works with. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.
  • On the web (CrazyGames): the ads are run by CrazyGames and its partners. See the CrazyGames Privacy Policy.
  • On our website (vgraphics.gr): Google AdSense, which shows the banner under the game, the ads between levels and the reward videos. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your earlier visits to our website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads based on your visits to our website and/or other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google's Ads Settings. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

These partners may automatically collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (and the rough location it reveals), device model, operating system, language, and how you interact with ads. They use it to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure results, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, to personalize ads. We don't receive this information about you personally.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent before your data is used for personalized ads, using Google's consent tool (see the Google Privacy Policy). On our website, Google's consent message appears when the game first opens. On iOS, ads are only personalized using your device's advertising identifier if you allow tracking when asked. If you say no, you may still see ads, but they won't be based on your activity in other apps.

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas or Virginia), you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for ads at any time. On Android and iOS: in the game, open Settings → Ad choices and choose "Don't sell or share my data". On our website: use the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link that Google's consent tool shows on the page. We then pass your choice on to our advertising partners.

Where we ask for your consent, you can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Ad choices, and on our website also with the "Ad choices" link under the game. You can also reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization at any time in your device's privacy or ads settings.

In-app purchases

If you buy something in the game (such as "Remove Ads" or coins), the payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies (Google, Apple). We never see your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation that the purchase was made.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

We don't hold your real name or contact details, and analytics data isn't tied to them. You can see your username, picture and results in the game, change your picture, and delete all of them as described under "Deleting your data". If you'd like anything else deleted or have a question about your data, email us and we'll help as far as we can. For data collected by our advertising partners, use the links above or your device's ad settings. Firebase services are provided by Google; see Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling information differently, we will update this page and the date at the top.
