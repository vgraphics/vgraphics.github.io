How to play

  1. Find an arrow whose path to the edge is clear.
  2. Tap it, and it snakes off the board.
  3. Every arrow you clear can free others. Keep going until the board is empty.
  4. Mind your hearts: tapping a blocked arrow costs one of three.

A new twist every few worlds

From level 21 a new obstacle arrives every 20 to 30 levels and then stays, mixed in with the ones before it. Each one starts on an easy level built to teach it, with a hand that shows you the move.

  1. Level 21

    Rocks & bombs

    Clear the arrow guarding a bomb's path, then blast its rock.

  2. Level 41

    Colour gates

    Only their own colour gets through, then they open.

  3. Level 61

    Mirrors

    Turn an arrow's path by 90 degrees.

  4. Level 81

    Level-2 rocks

    Two bombs, usually from two sides.

  5. Level 101

    Portals

    In one, out of its pair, across the board.

  6. Level 131

    Ice

    Melts as arrows leave. Its number is just enough.

  7. Level 161

    Level-3 rocks

    The toughest stone: three bomb hits.

  8. Level 191

    Paint tiles

    Fly over one and take its colour.

  9. Level 221

    Shutters

    Hide the arrows under them, even from powerups, until they roll up.

  10. Level 251

    Two-colour gates

    Two colours, and the big symbol goes first.

  11. Level 281

    Chain bombs & rock walls

    One blast runs along a whole wall.

  12. Level 311

    Colour switches

    Press one to open every gate of its colour, except two-colour gates.
