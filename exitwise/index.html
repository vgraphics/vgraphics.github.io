How to play

  1. Find an arrow whose path to the edge is clear.
  2. Tap it, and it snakes off the board.
  3. Every arrow you clear can free others. Keep going until the board is empty.
  4. Mind your hearts: tapping a blocked arrow costs one of three.

A new twist every few worlds

From level 21 a new obstacle arrives every 20 to 30 levels and then stays, mixed in with the ones before it. Each one starts on an easy level built to teach it, with a hand that shows you the move.

  1. Level 21Rocks & bombsClear the arrow guarding a bomb's path, then blast its rock.
  2. Level 41Colour gatesOnly their own colour gets through, then they open.
  3. Level 61MirrorsTurn an arrow's path by 90 degrees.
  4. Level 81Level-2 rocksTwo bombs, usually from two sides.
  5. Level 101PortalsIn one, out of its pair, across the board.
  6. Level 131IceMelts as arrows leave. Its number is just enough.
  7. Level 161Level-3 rocksThe toughest stone: three bomb hits.
  8. Level 191Paint tilesFly over one and take its colour.
  9. Level 221ShuttersHide the arrows under them, even from powerups, until they roll up.
  10. Level 251Two-colour gatesTwo colours, and the big symbol goes first.
  11. Level 281Chain bombs & rock wallsOne blast runs along a whole wall.
  12. Level 311Colour switchesPress one to open every gate of its colour, except two-colour gates.
