How to play

  1. 1

    Spot a free arrow

    Find an arrow whose path to the edge is clear.

  2. 2

    Tap it

    Tap it, and it snakes off the board.

  3. 3

    Clear the board

    Every arrow you clear can free others. Keep going until the board is empty.

  4. 4

    Mind your hearts

    Tapping a blocked arrow costs one of your three hearts.

A new twist every few worlds

From level 21 a new obstacle arrives every 20 to 30 levels and then stays, mixed in with the ones before it. Each one starts on an easy level built to teach it, with a hand that shows you the move.

  1. JungleLevels 21–40

    • 21RockStops every arrow until its bombs blow it up.
    • 21Bomb arrowFlies to its rock and blows it up, once its path is clear.

  2. VolcanoLevels 41–80

    • 41Colour gateLets only its own colour through, then opens for good.
    • 61MirrorTurns an arrow's path by 90 degrees.

  3. DesertLevels 81–100

    • 81Level-2 rockA tougher rock that takes two bomb hits.

  4. LagoonLevels 101–140

    • 101PortalCarries an arrow's path across the board to its pair.
    • 131IceA block with a number that melts as arrows leave.

  5. Autumn woodsLevels 161–200

    • 161Level-3 rockThe toughest rock: three bomb hits.
    • 191Paint tileGives an arrow that passes over it its colour.

  6. Crystal cavesLevels 221–260

    • 221ShutterA roller door that hides arrows until it rolls up.
    • 251Two-colour gateA gate that needs two colours, in order.

  7. Clockwork townLevels 281–320

    • 281Chain bomb & rock wallsOne blast runs along a whole wall of touching rocks.
    • 311Keys and doorsA key unlocks every door of its colour at once, but not two-colour gates.
