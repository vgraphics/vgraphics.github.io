Refund policy

You can have your money back for anything you bought in Palermo Noir on the web, if you ask within 14 days. No reason needed. This page says how to ask, and what a refund takes away. In plain words. At launch the only item on sale is the Host Pass; what this page says about sets, the supporter pack, looks and coins applies from the day each goes on sale.

The 14 days

Anything you buy on the web, the Host Pass and, when they go on sale, a set of looks or the supporter pack, can be refunded on request within 14 days of the day you bought it, the date on your receipt. You do not have to give a reason. The refund is the full price you paid, VAT included.

The web sale is made by Paddle, our merchant of record, which pays the money back. Our terms of sale say who sells what.

How to ask

  1. Write to support@vgraphics.gr from any address.
  2. Give the order number from Paddle's receipt email, and your VHub name.
  3. We pass your request to Paddle, which returns the money the way you paid. How soon your bank shows it is up to your bank.
  4. The item is taken off your VHub account.

You can also ask Paddle directly, with the help link in its receipt email.

What a refund takes away

A refund removes the item you got for that money:

  • Host Pass: the pass ends. A room that is already open keeps it until the room closes, so a game night is not cut in half. The next room you open shows ads again.
  • A set (when sets are on sale): its four looks go back to the free ones. A look you were wearing is taken off, and the free avatar shows from the next time you join a room.
  • Supporter pack (when it is on sale): only what that pack gave you is removed. Whatever another purchase of yours gave you stays.

Nothing else about your account changes, and a refund does not affect your VHub name, level, friends or progress.

Coins

Coins and single looks apply from the day they go on sale.

  • A look bought with coins is not refunded, and its coins are not returned. The look stays yours.
  • A coin pack exists only in the phone apps, so the store that sold it refunds it (see in the phone apps). When a pack is refunded, its coins are taken back. If you have already spent them, your coin balance can go below zero until you have earned them back, and you cannot spend coins until then.

Disputed payments

If you ask your bank to reverse a payment and it does, the item is removed in the same way as in a refund. Please write to us first: it is quicker, and it spares you a dispute.

In the phone apps

What you buy in the Android app, items and coin packs, is sold by Google Play, so Google Play's own refund rules apply and you ask Google Play for the refund, from your Play order history. When Google refunds a purchase, VHub removes the item from your account in the same way as above. Later, an iOS app would work the same way with Apple.

Your legal rights

The items are digital content, delivered at once. Paddle's checkout and buyer terms deal with the statutory right of withdrawal for digital content, which can end once delivery has begun with your consent. This refund policy is separate and stays on top of it: you can still ask within the 14 days. It never takes away your rights under consumer law, for example when an item does not arrive or does not work as described. In that case, write to us.

Changes

We may change this policy for purchases made later. A purchase keeps the refund terms it was made under. When this policy changes, we update this page and its date at the top.
