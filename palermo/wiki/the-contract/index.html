The Lawyer's briefcase

A host variant: the Lawyer gets a secret client and wins too if they're alive at the end.

Card
A host variant
When
From the deal
Target
A secret client
Seen
Secret

What it does

With this variant on, the Lawyer plays Solo with this one card instead of the briefcase: at the deal he gets a secret client.

If the client is alive at the end, the Lawyer wins too, whoever else wins.
