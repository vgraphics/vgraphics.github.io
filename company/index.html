About VGraphics

VGraphics is an independent game and app studio in Thessaloniki, Greece. This website and the games and apps on it are published by VGRAPHICS SINGLE MEMBER P.C., a company registered in Greece.

The studio

VGraphics is one person. The games, the apps, the servers behind them, the release tooling and this website are designed, built and shipped here, so when you write to us, the person who answers is the person who made the thing you're writing about.

The company was founded in January 2025. Today it makes a puzzle game, a live wallpaper for Android and a party game for the living room, with one account, VHub, that ties them together.

What we make

Exitwise

An arrow puzzle: an arrow slides off the board only when nothing blocks its path, so the order is everything. 600 levels in 17 worlds, rooms to race friends and a global ranking. On Android, and playable in your browser.

About Exitwise

Speedometer Live Wallpaper

A car dashboard for your Android home screen: battery power, processor clock or GPS speed on a big gauge, in 19 styles. Its real engine runs on this site, so you can try every style first.

About Speedometer

Palermo Noir

A party game of killers and townsfolk for 6 to 14 players, each with a phone, around one TV. In the works, and coming to the web first.

About Palermo Noir

VHub

One account for every VGraphics game and app: a name and picture, a level, achievements and friends.

Open VHub

How we work

Puzzle levels come from fixed seeds, so every player gets the same board, and each one is tested to be solvable before it ships. The web versions run the same code as the apps rather than a copy made for the web. Privacy policies are short enough to read, most pages on this site collect nothing at all, and the web games ask before counting statistics and, in Europe, before showing personalised ads.

We write about how the games and apps are made in the devlog. For work for clients, from Android apps to multiplayer backends, see what we build. And for anything else, email support@vgraphics.gr: every email gets read.
