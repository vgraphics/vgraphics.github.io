Purchases and coins

What happens to your data when you buy the Host Pass or looks in Palermo Noir, or earn and spend its coins. This is a part of the game's privacy policy, and of VHub's. The Host Pass goes on sale first; what it says about looks and coins applies from the day they go on sale. In plain words.

Last updated 28 October 2026

Draft for George's review: the text of a new part of two privacy policies, not in force yet, not linked from the site.

In short

  • No card details with usPaddle and Google take the payment. We never see your card.

  • Others see only what you wearNot what you paid, and not what else you own.

Who takes the payment

  • On the web, Paddle (Paddle.com Market Limited) is the seller and takes the payment. It collects what it needs for that, such as your email address, country and payment details, and uses them for the sale, the VAT, the receipt, fraud checks and refunds, under its own privacy policy. It is responsible for that data on its own account. It does not give us your card details.
  • In the Android app, Google Play takes the payment under Google's privacy policy, and likewise gives us no card details. With each purchase the app gives Google a one-way scrambled form of your VHub identifier (its SHA-256 hash), which Google keeps with the purchase so that it reaches only the account that made it; your identifier cannot be read back from it. Google gives the app a purchase token, which the app sends to VHub, and VHub checks it with Google before the item is yours. An iOS app, once there is one, would use Apple in the same way.
  • The checkout carries your VHub identifier, so the purchase lands on the right account. Paddle or Google tells VHub that the order was paid or refunded, with the item, the price and currency, the time and the store's order number.

What VHub keeps

VHub, our player network, keeps these on your account, in Google Firebase in Belgium, as VHub's privacy policy describes:

  • Your orders: the item, the store (Paddle, Google Play or coins), the price and currency, the dates of the payment and of any refund, and the store's order number. Of a Google Play purchase VHub keeps no purchase token, only a one-way hash of it with the account it belongs to, so that one purchase never serves two accounts.
  • What you own, and which looks you have put on.
  • Your coins: your balance, and a list of each time coins were earned, spent, bought or taken back, with the game, the reason, the amount and the time.
  • A check that your account is signed in (not a guest) and that your age group is 16 or over, before an order is accepted.

Palermo Noir's own Firebase project receives only what the game needs to show you: whether you have the Host Pass, whether you are a supporter, and which looks you wear.

What others see

  • The players in your room, and strangers at an online table, see the looks you wear: your portrait, wax seal, card back and name plate.
  • The TV of a room whose host has the Host Pass shows one line in the lobby, such as "No ads tonight, thanks to Maria's Host Pass", with the name the host plays under.
  • Nobody sees what you paid, which orders you made, what else you own or how many coins you have.

Your age

Buying with money needs an age group of 16 or over, which the game works out from the year of birth it asks for once and does not keep (see your age). Accounts aged 13 to 15 can earn and spend coins only: they get no checkout, no offers and no nudges to buy.

How long, and deleting

  • Your purchases and coins are kept as long as your VHub account exists, so that signing in anywhere brings them back.
  • When you delete your VHub account, your owned items, worn looks, coins and coin history are deleted with it, and the deletion screen says so. The record of each order stays, no longer linked to your VHub account, for as long as accounting law and late refunds need it: it holds the item, the price, the dates and the store's order number. That number is not a name, but Paddle or Google can trace it to their own records of the sale.
  • Paddle and Google keep their own records of the sale, as the tax and accounting laws require of them, under their own policies. VGraphics keeps its accounting records of what they pay it for as long as Greek law requires.
  • You can ask for a copy of what VHub keeps about your purchases with the data download in VHub, or by writing to support@vgraphics.gr.

Analytics

Only if you allow analytics, and never for players under 16, the game counts that the store was opened, which item was looked at, that a purchase was started, completed, cancelled or failed, that purchases were restored, and that a refund or a look change happened, with the item and the price. These counts carry no name or email address. See analytics and error reports.

Why we may

Legal basis: keeping your orders and items so that we can deliver what you bought, and give or take it back after a refund, is our contract with you (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Keeping accounting records is a legal duty (Article 6(1)(c)). Analytics rely on your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time. Paddle and Google may handle data outside the EU, under the safeguards their policies describe.

Your other rights, and how to use them, are in the Palermo Noir privacy policy. The terms of sale and the refund policy say what you buy and how to get it refunded.
