The studio

One person, a long to-do list, high standards

VGraphics is a single-member private company (ΙΚΕ) founded in 2025 in Thessaloniki, Greece. We design, build and publish our own games and apps.

Fair by design

Levels come from fixed seeds, so everyone plays the same puzzle, and every one is tested to be solvable. No luck, just logic.

Made for short breaks

Quick to pick up, easy to pause, and playable offline: on a commute, in a queue, or with a coffee.
