Speedometer Live Wallpaper

The big dial shows power, CPU clock or your GPS speed. Around it: battery, memory, signal and Wi-Fi, with warning lights and a digital clock. Pick from 19 dashboard styles: Classic, Steel and Graphite are free, and the others, from a 1960s walnut console to a fighter jet's head-up display, are €1.19 each.

  • One dial, three jobsPower in watts, CPU clock or GPS speed. Tap the gauge or use the Quick Settings tile to switch.
  • Three styles free, the rest €1.19Each style is a one-time purchase, or €5.99 unlocks them all, the ones still to come too (euro-area prices with VAT). They come back by themselves on a new phone with the same Google account.
  • Easy on the batteryThe gauges redraw only while a needle moves, and nothing runs while the wallpaper is hidden.
  • No ads on your wallpaperA small banner shows inside the app only, never on your home screen.
