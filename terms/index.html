What you can post and how we moderate

These rules cover VHub, the player account in every VGraphics game and app and at vgraphics.gr/hub. VHub is for players aged 13 and over. Players under 13 can play, but none of what follows is open to them.

What players share in VHub

  • Your name and picture. Every account starts with a name we make. You can change it once, and the name you choose is checked against a list of rude and reserved words. A picture is one of our preset pictures, or a photo you upload from a Google or Apple account; every photo is checked automatically before anyone sees it.
  • Moments. A moment is a short line VHub writes about your play, such as "reached level 100 in Exitwise" or "unlocked a gold badge". You don't write it: it comes from what the games report to VHub. Only your friends see your moments. You can stop sharing them in Settings ("Share my moments with friends"), delete one, or turn its comments off.
  • Reactions. Your friends can react to a moment with one of four reactions: GG, heart, fire or star.
  • Comments. A comment is one phrase picked from VHub's own list, such as "GG!" or "Race you!". Nobody types anything. Only the person who owns the post and their friends can comment. You can post each phrase once on a post, at most 3 comments by one person on one post, and at most 50 comments a day.
  • No chat, no messages. Players can't send each other words they typed, anywhere in VHub.

The rules

When you use VHub, don't:

  • bully, harass, threaten or keep picking on another player, for example by posting phrases at them over and over, or making new accounts to reach someone who blocked you;
  • choose a name or picture that is sexual, violent or hateful, that pretends to be someone else, or that shows where someone lives, their school or how to reach them;
  • upload a picture of someone else without asking them, or a picture you have no right to use;
  • spam, scam, or sell anything;
  • report players who did nothing wrong, or get others to do it with you;
  • cheat, or use more than one account to get around a block, a limit or a sanction;
  • do anything illegal, or help someone else do it.

Nothing that sexualises a child, or tries to, is ever allowed. That includes asking a young player for pictures or how to reach them. An account that does this is banned for good and reported to the police. See our Child safety page.

Reporting

  • In the game or on the hub: open the ⋯ menu on a profile, a name, a picture or a moment and choose Report; on a comment, choose "Report player". You pick a reason from a list, and you can add a note that only VGraphics reads; the player you report never sees it, or who reported them. You are asked to confirm before a report is sent.
  • A report counts when it comes from an account at least a day old, once per account.
  • Anyone, with or without an account, can tell us about something illegal in VHub by emailing info@vgraphics.gr. Please tell us: where it is (the player's name or the address of their profile, and what you saw), why you believe it is illegal, and your name and email address. You don't have to give your name when you report child sexual abuse material. Please also say that you believe what you tell us is true. We confirm that we got your email, and tell you what we decided.
  • We look at reports about children's safety first.

Blocking and hiding

  • Block, in the same ⋯ menu, ends your friendship, stops friend requests and invites both ways, and hides each of you from the other everywhere in VHub: names, pictures, moments, reactions and comments. You are asked to confirm first.
  • Hide a friend's moments from a moment's ⋯ menu: you stay friends, and they aren't told. Undo it from their profile.
  • On your own posts you can delete any comment, and turn comments off for one post, or for all of them in Settings ("Who can comment": Friends or Nobody).

What happens by itself, and what a person decides

Some steps run by themselves, the same way for everyone:

  • a name is checked against our word list when it is chosen;
  • a photo is checked by Google Cloud Vision before anyone sees it, and refused if it shows nudity, violence or similar content;
  • when three players report the same name or picture, it is removed; an uploader who loses two pictures this way can't upload any more;
  • when three different players report the same player within 30 days, that player can't comment for 7 days, and the case comes to us;
  • the comment limits above.

Everything else is decided by a person at VGraphics. We look at each case that comes to us: we put back what was removed by mistake, confirm a removal, or apply a sanction. We don't use AI to decide reports.

Sanctions

Depending on what happened, how serious it was and whether it happened before, we may:

  • remove a name or picture;
  • stop a player's comments, or their uploads;
  • mute an account: no friend requests, rooms, invites, reactions or comments;
  • suspend it: all of that, and no changes to its name or picture;
  • ban it: all of that, and the games may stop it playing.

A sanction can cover VHub everywhere or one game, and lasts a set time or for good. We remove illegal content, and an account that shares child sexual abuse material, or grooms a child, is banned for good at once.

We tell you why

When we remove something of yours or limit your account, VHub's inbox tells you: what we did, for how long, why (the facts, such as "three players reported you", and the rule above it broke), whether it happened by itself or a person decided it, and how to appeal. We say nothing only where the law or an authority tells us not to.

Appeals

If you think we got it wrong, you can appeal within 6 months: open a help ticket in the game or app (Settings, Help) or at vgraphics.gr/hub/support and say which decision you mean, or email info@vgraphics.gr. The same goes if you reported something and disagree with what we decided. A person at VGraphics reviews every appeal, and we answer as soon as we can, usually within 7 days. If we were wrong, we undo it at once.

You can also go to the courts (see Responsibility and law), or complain to the Hellenic Telecommunications and Post Commission (EETT, www.eett.gr), which oversees online services in Greece under the EU's Digital Services Act.

Contact for users and authorities

Players, and authorities in the EU, reach us about VHub at info@vgraphics.gr, in English or Greek. This is also our contact point under the EU's Digital Services Act and for removal orders under Regulation (EU) 2021/784.
