What is never sold

Roles, a better chance at a role, modes, player slots and host rules: all of them stay free for everyone. Nothing that changes a game is sold. Nothing random is sold either: you always see what you buy, and its price, before you pay. There are no mystery boxes and no packs with unknown contents, for euros or for coins.

Who may buy

  • Buying with money needs a signed-in VHub account (not a guest) whose age group is 16 or over. The game asks for a year of birth once and keeps only the age group.
  • Players aged 13 to 15 pay nothing in euros. Once looks and coins are on sale, they earn and spend coins like everyone else, see the sets without a Buy button, and get no offers.
  • Spending coins (once coins are on sale) needs a signed-in VHub account too, so that a look is never lost with a guest browser.
  • Palermo Noir is for ages 13 and up. Under 13, the game does not open.

Paying and delivery

On the web you choose an item in the game's store, or on its page here, and Paddle's checkout opens. You pay with a method Paddle offers. The checkout carries your VHub account, so the item arrives on the account you were signed in with. The TV page of the game never sells anything: no checkout, no prices.

Everything is digital and is delivered at once: as soon as Paddle confirms the payment, the item is on your VHub account, normally within moments.

Because of that, the statutory right of withdrawal for digital content can end once delivery has begun with your consent (Directive 2011/83/EU on consumer rights, Article 16(m), and the Greek consumer protection law). Paddle's checkout and buyer terms deal with this. It does not touch our own refund policy, which is more generous: you can still ask for a refund within 14 days (see refunds).

What you get

  • A personal right to use the item in Palermo Noir, with your VHub account, for as long as the game is offered. You may not sell, share or transfer it.
  • Looks are for show (from the day they go on sale). A look belongs to the player, never to a role: it is drawn the same way, with the same timing and sound, whatever role you hold, and it changes nothing anyone sees or hears about the game. Others at the table, and online, see what you wear.
  • It is kept on your VHub account, not on a phone. Sign in on any phone or computer and your purchases are back. Deleting your VHub account deletes what you bought; the deletion screen says so.
  • A refund or a reversed payment removes the item (see refunds).

Coins

Coins and single looks apply from the day they go on sale; until then there is nothing to earn or spend.

  • Coins are earned by playing. A finished game earns 30 coins, a win 15 more, and the first game finished each day another 30, up to 300 coins a day from Palermo Noir.
  • Coins are VHub's: coins earned in another VGraphics game that uses VHub coins can be spent here, and the other way round. They do not expire.
  • Coins have no cash value. They cannot be exchanged for money or given to another player.
  • Every coin price is a multiple of 300, the smallest coin pack, so you can always buy exactly what you need. Each price shows its value in euros under it.
  • Coins spent on a look are not refunded, and the look stays yours.

Refunds

Ask within 14 days of buying on the web, give no reason, and you get your money back through Paddle. The item is removed from your account. The refund policy has the details: how to ask, what a refund takes away, coins and the phone apps.

In the phone apps

  • In the Android app, the items on sale there and the coin packs are sold by Google Play under Google's terms, and Google Play's refund rules apply to them. Later, an iOS app would sell through Apple in the same way.
  • The same items, at the same prices in euros, are sold on every platform. What you buy anywhere is kept on your VHub account, so a pass bought on the web works in the app, and the other way round.
  • Coin packs exist only in the phone apps.

Help

If an item does not arrive, or does not work as described, write to support@vgraphics.gr and we will put it right or refund you. For a question about a payment or a receipt from the web, you can also use the help link in Paddle's receipt email.

Responsibility and law

Your rights as a consumer, including your rights when digital content does not work as described, stay as the law gives them. Nothing here takes them away.

Greek law applies to these terms, and the courts of Thessaloniki, Greece, decide any dispute about them, without taking away the protection, or the right to go to your own courts, that the law of the country you live in gives you as a consumer. If we cannot settle a complaint, you can turn to an out-of-court dispute body in Greece, such as the Consumer Ombudsman. The rest of our terms of use apply to this website, and our privacy policy and what we keep about your purchases explain your data.

Changes

We may add items, change prices and change these terms. What you have already bought keeps the price and the terms it was bought under. When these terms change, we update this page and its date at the top.
