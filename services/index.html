How we work

  1. Tell us what you needA few lines are enough: what it should do, who it's for, which platforms, and when you need it.
  2. Get a planWe reply with questions, then a plan for a first version you can put in people's hands.
  3. Try it as it growsYou get test builds on your phone or in your browser as the work goes, not only at the end.
  4. Launch and look after itWe prepare the store listing and the privacy answers, release it, and keep an eye on crashes.
  • You talk to the person who builds itVGraphics is one engineer, so nothing gets lost between sales, design and code.
  • Privacy and store rules from day oneConsent forms where the law asks for them, privacy policies in plain words, and data-safety answers that match the code.
  • Tests where they countEvery one of Exitwise's 600 levels is proven solvable by tests, and its security rules are tested before they go live.
