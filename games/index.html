Exitwise

Arrow Escape Puzzle

Tap an arrow and it slides off the board, but only if nothing is in its way. Clear every arrow to win. The boards grow and change shape as you go, and a new obstacle arrives every few worlds: bombs, colour gates, mirrors, portals, ice, shutters and more.

Party games, on the way

One phone each, one TV for the room. The game deals, keeps score and runs the show, so nobody has to sit out to host. Both will play in Greek and English. Palermo Noir is being built now and Quizfall is still in design, so there are no dates yet.

Coming soon

Palermo Noir

A hidden-role mafia party game in the style of the classic house game. A host opens a room, friends join from their phones, and the game deals everyone a secret role. The TV shows the town, and an automatic narrator runs every night and day, so all of you get to play.

  • Phones as controllers
  • The TV as the main screen
  • An automatic narrator

In design

Quizfall

Who will be the last one standing?

A party quiz. Everyone in the room answers the same questions on their phone, with a few helps that run out. A wrong answer knocks you out, and the last one standing wins. The TV shows the question, the room's ranking and everyone's helps.

  • Same question for everyone
  • Helps that run out
  • The ranking on the TV

Both games are built on VHub's party kit: the lobby, joining by code or QR, the TV screen, the host's rules and the narrator.
