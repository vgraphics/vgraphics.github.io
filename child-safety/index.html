Child safety

VGraphics makes games and apps, and VHub, the player account they share. This page is our standard against child sexual abuse and exploitation. It covers VHub at vgraphics.gr/hub and in every VGraphics game and app, including those on Google Play.

Our standard

We do not allow anything that sexualises children, anywhere in our games, apps or VHub. That includes:

  • child sexual abuse material: pictures or videos, real, drawn or made with AI;
  • grooming: trying to gain a child's trust in order to abuse them;
  • asking a child for pictures, how to reach them, or to meet;
  • threatening to share someone's pictures to get more pictures or money (sextortion);
  • sharing such material, or links to it.

An account that does any of this is banned for good, and we report it to the police.

How VHub is built to keep children safe

  • 13 and over. Players under 13 can play, but get no name, picture, friends, invites, moments, reactions or comments.
  • No chat, no messages. Players can't send each other words they typed. Comments are phrases from our own list, and none is about a person's body, looks, age or where they are, or about meeting up.
  • Friends only. Moments, reactions and comments are for friends. Other players see only a name, a picture, a level and badges.
  • Pictures are checked before anyone sees them. Only accounts signed in with Google or Apple can upload a picture. It is copied without its hidden details (such as where it was taken) and checked automatically for nudity and similar content.
  • Players aged 13 to 15 can stop all comments on their posts with one tap.
  • Block hides two players from each other everywhere in VHub.

Report it

  • In the game or on the hub: open the ⋯ menu on a profile, name, picture, moment or comment and choose Report. Reports about children's safety are looked at first.
  • By email, with or without an account: info@vgraphics.gr. You don't have to give your name. Please don't send the material itself: send the player's name or the address of their profile, and what you saw.
  • If a child is in danger now, call 112.
  • In Greece, you can also report to the Hellenic Police's Cyber Crime Division (11188, cyberalert.gr) or SafeLine (www.safeline.gr). Children and parents can call The Smile of the Child on 1056 or 116 111.
  • Elsewhere, contact your local police or your country's hotline at www.inhope.org. In the United States, report to NCMEC's CyberTipline (report.cybertip.org).

What we do when we find abuse

  • We take it out of everyone's sight at once.
  • We ban the account, and any account used to get around the ban.
  • We report it to the Hellenic Police's Cyber Crime Division and, where it fits, to the hotline or police of the country involved, including NCMEC in the United States. When a child's life or safety may be at risk, we tell the police at once.
  • We keep a locked copy, seen by nobody, only for as long as the police need it, then delete it. We never share it with anyone else.
  • We help the police with their investigation, as the law allows.

We follow the child-safety laws that apply to us, including Greek and EU law.

Child safety contact

Our contact for child safety is info@vgraphics.gr. A person at VGraphics reads it, and answers questions about how we prevent child sexual abuse, from players, parents, Google, Apple and the authorities, in English or Greek.
