Designs

The Designs screen

Swipe through the designs, use one, or buy Future.

What it does

Open the app and tap Designs. Each design is shown as a preview drawn by the wallpaper itself at your phone's shape, with its name, a short description and its status: In use, Free, Owned or its price.

How it works for you

  • Tap Use this design to switch: the wallpaper changes at once.
  • Tap Buy for €0.99 to buy Future through Google Play.

Good to know

  • If a payment is still pending, or Google Play isn't available, the screen tells you.
