The Styles screen

Style

All 19 styles, drawn live: use one, buy one, pick its layout, or build your own with 48 widgets.

How it works

  1. 1

    Tap Use this style to switch: the wallpaper changes at once. If Speedometer isn't your live wallpaper yet, a sheet offers Set as wallpaper, so you see the style on your home screen; Not now keeps the style for later. The same sheet comes up once after you buy a style or Speedometer Plus, or start a 24 h trial with a short video, as soon as Google Play's window or the video has closed.

  2. 2

    Tap Buy to buy a style once through Google Play, or Every style for Speedometer Plus, a monthly or yearly subscription: every style, including the styles still to come, and no ads. Google Play shows the price for your country.

  3. 3

    Under Layouts, pick one of the style's own, a ready-made one, one of yours (Mine) or one from the market, and tap Use this layout. Edit layout opens it in the layout builder: one of yours changes in every style, any other becomes your own copy and the original stays as it is. New layout, the + card at the end of the row, starts one of your own: from the layout on show, or blank.

  4. 4

    Show QR for this look on the try page of vgraphics.gr puts a style and layout in a QR code: scan it with your phone and the app opens on that style, the layout shown as if you had picked it. Tap Use this layout to keep it, or Buy for a style you don't own yet.

  5. 5

    In the layout builder, Add widget brings in a widget, a drag moves it and its red handle resizes it, and Bring to front and Send to back stack widgets that overlap. The chips under the layout list its layers, front first: drag one by its handle (or hold it and drag) to move that widget up or down the stack. The padlock toggle beside a chosen widget's name locks it in place while you arrange the rest. Pinch the layout, or use the buttons in its corner, to zoom in on the details. Preview in shows it in any style, even one you don't own yet. Save layout keeps it under Mine, and the style's page comes back with your new layout on show, the Layouts row scrolled to its card (with animations off, it jumps there); Save and publish to the market shares it.

  6. 6

    Marketplace, a card on the Styles screen and a button on Home, opens every layout drivers have shared, across every style and every screen shape. Each card is drawn in the style it was made with and shows that style's name (Made with, for a style you don't own yet), the shapes it's made for, and its maker's VHub picture and name. Sort by Popular, New or Top rated, filter by style, by shape (Phone, Tall / Flip, Foldable or Tablet) and by what it shows, and tap the heart to keep one under Favourites, on this phone. Open a layout to use it; in a style you don't own yet, it opens as a preview, then Buy or a trial. Report sends one to VGraphics™ to check. Only layouts that passed the checks are listed, and you can look without signing in.

  7. 7

    Add widget lists the 48 widgets by section, each with a live preview of how the style draws it: Gauges (the main gauge, battery, RAM, signal, Wi-Fi, lights, the phone's status meters, a backplate and more), On the road (trip distance, average and top speed, drive time, a trip computer, a 0–100 timer, G-force, the odometer, climb rate, the Map and Next turn), Where you are (heading, altitude, tilt, sunrise and sunset, the moon, weather, air pressure), The phone (battery time, data speed, storage, steps today), Your day (the clock, date, the date and time together and a second clock, next alarm and event, Now Playing, music buttons, volume, calls and messages) and Your photos (a photo frame, an instant print and a round photo). Every style shows every widget, each in its own look, and a widget reads only while it's on your wallpaper.

  8. 8

    The Map comes with Speedometer Pro: the OpenStreetMap streets round you, heading-up and zooming out as you speed up, as a round nav screen or a strip, in each style's own colours. A fading trail shows the road you've just driven, and while Google Maps or Waze guides you, the way to your next turn lights up. It covers Greece for now.

  9. 9

    A widget that needs your location, your steps or your calendar asks Android for it when you add it; the weather asks only for your approximate location. Pick the second clock's city, your step goal and the tilt's level in Settings, under Widgets.

  10. 10

    Now Playing, the music buttons, Calls and messages and Next turn need notification access: the app takes you there when you add one. In the previews they show a sample song and counts; on your wallpaper, until the access is on, Now Playing reads MUSIC AND CALLS ARE OFF and Calls and messages reads CALLS AND MESSAGES ARE OFF.

What it shows

Open the app and tap Styles. Your styles come first, then the rest, each drawn live by the wallpaper itself with its name and its state: In use, Free, Owned or Locked. Tap one for its page: a live demo you can pause, Power, CPU and Speed buttons, its description and its layouts.

Good to know

  • If a payment is still pending, or Google Play isn't available, the screen tells you.
  • Only the Weather and Map widgets send anything: a rough location, never your exact point. The weather's, rounded to about 10 km, goes to MET Norway (api.met.no) for the forecast; the map asks our map host for the squares of about 2 km round you. Weather data from MET Norway, under the Norwegian Licence for Open Government Data and CC BY 4.0.
  • The music buttons work where your home screen passes taps to the wallpaper; Samsung's home screen doesn't.
