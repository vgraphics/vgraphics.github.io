The Styles screen

Style

All 19 styles, drawn live: use one, buy one, pick its layout, or build your own with 42 widgets.

How it works

  1. 1

    Tap Use this style to switch: the wallpaper changes at once.

  2. 2

    Tap Buy to buy a style once through Google Play, or Every style for Speedometer Plus, a monthly or yearly subscription: every style, including the styles still to come, and no ads. Google Play shows the price for your country.

  3. 3

    Under Layouts, pick one of the style's own, a ready-made one, one of yours (Mine) or one from the market, and tap Use this layout. Edit layout opens it in the layout builder: one of yours changes in every style, any other becomes your own copy and the original stays as it is. New layout starts one from scratch.

  4. 4

    In the layout builder, Add widget brings in a widget, a drag moves it and its red handle resizes it, and Bring to front and Send to back stack widgets that overlap. The padlock toggle beside a chosen widget's name locks it in place while you arrange the rest. Pinch the layout, or use the buttons in its corner, to zoom in on the details. Preview in shows it in any style, even one you don't own yet. Save layout keeps it under Mine; Save and publish to the market shares it.

  5. 5

    Add widget lists the 42 widgets by section, each with a live preview of how the style draws it: Gauges (the main gauge, battery, RAM, signal, Wi-Fi, lights, an info display, a backplate and more), On the road (trip distance, average and top speed, drive time, a trip computer, a 0–100 timer, G-force, the odometer, climb rate), Where you are (heading, altitude, tilt, sunrise and sunset, the moon, weather, air pressure), The phone (battery time, data speed, storage, steps today) and Your day (the clock, date and a second clock, next alarm and event, Now Playing, music buttons, volume, calls and messages). Every style shows every widget, each in its own look, and a widget reads only while it's on your wallpaper.

  6. 6

    A widget that needs your location, your steps or your calendar asks Android for it when you add it; the weather asks only for your approximate location. Pick the second clock's city, your step goal and the tilt's level in Settings, under Widgets.

  7. 7

    Now Playing, the music buttons and Calls and messages need notification access: the app takes you there when you add one. In the previews they show a sample song and counts; on your wallpaper, until the access is on, Now Playing reads MUSIC AND CALLS ARE OFF and Calls and messages reads CALLS AND MESSAGES ARE OFF.

What it shows

Open the app and tap Styles. Your styles come first, then the rest, each drawn live by the wallpaper itself with its name and its state: In use, Free, Owned or Locked. Tap one for its page: a live demo you can pause, Power, CPU and Speed buttons, its description and its layouts.

Good to know

  • If a payment is still pending, or Google Play isn't available, the screen tells you.
  • Only the weather sends anything: your rough location, rounded to about 10 km, goes to MET Norway (api.met.no) for the forecast. Weather data from MET Norway, under the Norwegian Licence for Open Government Data and CC BY 4.0.
  • The music buttons work where your home screen passes taps to the wallpaper; Samsung's home screen doesn't.
