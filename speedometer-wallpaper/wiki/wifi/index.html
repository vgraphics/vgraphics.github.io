Small gauges

Wi-Fi

Bottom left: Wi-Fi strength, status and link speed.

What it does

The Wi-Fi gauge shows the strength of your Wi-Fi signal.

Under it, a status line reads CONNECTED, NO NETWORK, MOBILE DATA or DISABLED. When connected, it also shows the link speed in Mbps.

Works with

Wi-Fi ON lightOn the Wi-Fi gauge: green on Wi-Fi, yellow on mobile data. SignalTop left: your mobile signal, from 0 to 4 bars.
