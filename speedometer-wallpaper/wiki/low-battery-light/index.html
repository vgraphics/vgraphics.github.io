Warning lights

Low battery light

On the big gauge: the battery is at or below your low level.

What it does

Lights when the battery is at or below the low battery level: 15 % unless you change it.

How it works for you

  • Settings → Leds → Low battery level changes the limit; Low battery led switches the light off.

Works with

LOW lightOn the battery gauge: the battery is low. BatteryBottom right: how full your battery is.
