How it works for you

  • Design: opens the Designs screen, and Music, calls and messages turns on notification access for the Future design.
  • Big gauge: the mode (power, CPU clock or GPS speed) and the speed unit (km/h or mph).
  • Date and time: time format, time separator, seconds, date order and date separator.
  • Leds: switch each warning light on or off, and set the low battery level and the low and high temperatures.
  • Privacy: Ad choices (where your region requires it) and the privacy policy.
