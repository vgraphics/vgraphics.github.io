Small gauges

Battery temperature

The little dial: from cold (C) to hot (H).

What it does

The small dial inside the battery gauge shows the battery's temperature, from the low temperature (C) to the high one (H).

How it works for you

  • Set what counts as low and high in Settings; the warning lights use the same limits.

Works with

High temperature lightOn the big gauge: the battery is hotter than your limit. Low temperature lightOn the big gauge: the battery is colder than your limit. BatteryBottom right: how full your battery is.
