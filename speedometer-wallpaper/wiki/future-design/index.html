Future design

The 2016 chrome cluster, rebuilt, for €0.99.

What it does

Future is one big chrome dial that follows the same three modes as Classic (power, CPU clock or GPS speed), with the value in its blue glass hub and battery and RAM arcs at the bottom.

Above it, the clock and date. Below it, the music area, the Bluetooth and Wi-Fi status, and how many calls you missed and messages you haven't read.

In landscape, on tablets and unfolded foldables, it becomes the 2016 two-dial cluster: the big gauge on the left, mobile signal and battery temperature on the right, music in the middle.

How it works for you

  • Buy it once on the Designs screen for €0.99. It's a one-time purchase, not a subscription.
  • Tap the dial, or use the Big gauge tile, to switch its mode.
  • The music and the call and message counts need notification access.
