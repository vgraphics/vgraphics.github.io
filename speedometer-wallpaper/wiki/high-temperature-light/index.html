High temperature light

Warning light

On the big gauge: the battery is hotter than your limit.

How it works

  1. 1

    Settings → Leds → High temperature sets the limit.

What it shows

Lights when the battery is hotter than the high temperature: 45 °C unless you change it.

Works with

Battery temperatureThe little dial: from cold (C) to hot (H). Low temperature lightOn the big gauge: the battery is colder than your limit.
