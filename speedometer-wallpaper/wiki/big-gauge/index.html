The big gauge

The big gauge

One dial, three jobs: power, CPU clock or GPS speed.

What it does

The big gauge shows one of three things: power, CPU clock or GPS speed. The digits above the needle show the same value as the needle.

How it works for you

  • Pick the mode in Settings → Big gauge → Mode, tap the gauge on your home screen, or use the Big gauge tile in Quick Settings to switch.

Works with

Power modeWatts going into or out of the battery. CPU clock modeHow fast the processor runs, as a share of its top speed. GPS speed modeYour speed in km/h or mph, with a red mark at your top speed. Big gauge tileA Quick Settings tile that switches the big gauge on any phone.
