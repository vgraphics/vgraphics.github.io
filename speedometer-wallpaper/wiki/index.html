Every gauge, light and setting

Every gauge, light, style and setting on your dashboard, and HUD mode, the car HUD that puts your speed on the windscreen: what each one shows, and how to set it up.

HUD mode

Your phone as a car HUD: lay it flat on the dashboard and read your speed on the windscreen.

HUD modeYour phone as a heads-up display for your car: lay it flat on the dashboard and read your GPS speed on the windscreen.

The big gauge

The dial in the middle. It shows one of three things; pick it in the style's settings (Customize), tap it on your home screen, or use the Big gauge tile.

The big gaugeOne dial, three jobs: power, CPU clock or GPS speed. Big gauge tileA Quick Settings tile that switches the big gauge on any phone. Power modeWatts going into or out of the battery. CPU clock modeHow fast the processor runs, as a share of its top speed. GPS speed modeYour speed in km/h or mph, with a red mark at your top speed.

Small gauges

The four dials around it, and the little temperature dial.

SignalTop left: your mobile signal, from 0 to 4 bars. RAMTop right: how much of the phone's memory is in use. Wi-FiBottom left: Wi-Fi strength, status and link speed. BatteryBottom right: how full your battery is. Battery temperatureThe little dial: from cold (C) to hot (H).

Warning lights

Lights on the big gauge and the small gauges. Each warning light can be switched off in Customize → Warning lamps.

Low battery lightOn the big gauge: the battery is at or below your low level. Bluetooth lightOn the big gauge: Bluetooth is on. High temperature lightOn the big gauge: your phone's battery is hotter than the limit you set, 45 °C unless you change it. Low temperature lightOn the big gauge: the battery is colder than your limit. Charging boltOn the battery gauge: the phone is charging. LOW lightOn the battery gauge: the battery is low. Wi-Fi ON lightOn the Wi-Fi gauge: green on Wi-Fi, yellow on mobile data.
