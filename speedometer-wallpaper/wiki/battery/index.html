Small gauges

Battery

Bottom right: how full your battery is.

What it does

The battery gauge shows how full your battery is. Its LOW light comes on at the low battery level, and the green bolt lights while the phone charges.

Works with

Battery temperatureThe little dial: from cold (C) to hot (H). LOW lightOn the battery gauge: the battery is low. Charging boltOn the battery gauge: the phone is charging. Power modeWatts going into or out of the battery.
