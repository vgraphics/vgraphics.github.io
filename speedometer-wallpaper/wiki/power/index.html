The big gauge

Power mode

Watts going into or out of the battery.

What it does

In power mode the needle shows the watts going into the battery (to the right, while charging) or out of it (to the left, with a minus).

How it works for you

  • Plug in your charger and watch the needle swing right: a quick way to see how fast a charger really is.

Works with

The big gaugeOne dial, three jobs: power, CPU clock or GPS speed. Charging boltOn the battery gauge: the phone is charging. BatteryBottom right: how full your battery is.
