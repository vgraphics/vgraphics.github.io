Restoring Future on a new phone

Bought once, back by itself on any phone with the same Google account.

What it does

Future is a one-time purchase tied to your Google account. The app checks your purchases with Google Play every time you open it, so on a new phone signed in with the same account, Future comes back by itself.

How it works for you

  • Install the app on the new phone, open it once, then pick Future on the Designs screen.
  • Your purchase is also remembered on the phone, so the wallpaper never waits for Google Play.

Good to know

  • A refunded purchase is removed the next time the app checks.
