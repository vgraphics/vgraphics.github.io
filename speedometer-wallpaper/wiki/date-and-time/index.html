Clock, settings & help

Date and time

The LCD under the big gauge.

What it does

The LCD under the big gauge shows the date and the time.

How it works for you

  • In Settings → Date and time you can set the time format (or follow the phone), the time separator, whether seconds show, the date order and the date separator.

SettingsEverything you can change. The big gaugeOne dial, three jobs: power, CPU clock or GPS speed.
