Warning lights

Low temperature light

On the big gauge: the battery is colder than your limit.

What it does

Lights when the battery is colder than the low temperature: 0 °C unless you change it.

How it works for you

  • Settings → Leds → Low temperature sets the limit.

Works with

Battery temperatureThe little dial: from cold (C) to hot (H). High temperature lightOn the big gauge: the battery is hotter than your limit.
