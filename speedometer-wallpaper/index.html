Your phone, as a car dashboard.

A live wallpaper with real gauges. The big dial shows your speed by GPS, the power your phone is drawing, or its CPU. Around it: battery, memory, signal and Wi-Fi, with warning lights and a digital clock.

Every needle is real

Each gauge reads your phone as it runs, a few times a second. Pick what the big dial shows in Settings.

  • 240Speed

    GPS speed in km/h or mph, while the wallpaper is on screen.

  • 4.2Power and CPU

    The watts your phone draws, or how hard its CPU works.

  • 064Battery and RAM

    Charge level and memory use on their own dials.

  • 433Signal and Wi-Fi

    Mobile signal strength and Wi-Fi link speed.

On your home screen

Rendered by the app itself. The dashboard fits phones and tablets of any shape.

Start your engine

Three steps from install to dashboard.

  1. Install Speedometer Live Wallpaper from Google Play.
  2. Open it and tap Set wallpaper, then confirm on your home screen.
  3. In Settings, choose what the big dial shows, your units, and which lights and clock to show.

Your readings stay on your phone

Nothing the gauges read is sent anywhere, and your location is used only for the speed in GPS mode. Read the privacy policy.
