Start your engine

Three steps to your dashboard

  1. Install Speedometer Live Wallpaper from Google Play.
  2. Open it and tap Set wallpaper, then confirm on your home screen.
  3. In Settings, choose what the big gauge shows, your units, and which lights and clock to show. In Designs, pick Classic or Future.
The big gauge

One dial, three jobs

Pick the mode in Settings, tap the gauge on your home screen, or use the Big gauge tile in Quick Settings, which switches it on every phone, Samsung's included. The digits above the needle show the same value.

  • Mode 1

    Power

    Watts going into the battery (to the right) or out of it (to the left, with a minus).

  • Mode 2

    CPU clock

    How fast the processor runs, as a share of its top speed. On phones that allow reading it.

  • Mode 3

    GPS speed

    Your speed in km/h or mph. A red mark shows the top speed of the current drive.

What every gauge shows

Tap a red dot on the dashboard, or open a part below.

  1. The small gauge at the top left shows your mobile signal, from 0 to 4 bars.

    More in the wiki →

  2. The small gauge at the top right shows how much of the phone's memory is in use.

    More in the wiki →

  3. It shows one of three things: power, CPU clock or GPS speed (see the modes above). Tap it on your home screen, or use the Big gauge tile in Quick Settings, to switch. The digits above the needle show the same value.

    More in the wiki →

  4. Four lights on the big gauge. Each one can be switched off in Settings.

    • Low battery: at or below the low battery level, 15 % unless you change it.
    • Bluetooth: while Bluetooth is on.
    • High temperature: when the battery is hotter than 45 °C, unless you change it.
    • Low temperature: when the battery is colder than 0 °C, unless you change it.
    More in the wiki →

  5. The LCD under the big gauge. The time format, date order, separators and seconds are all in Settings.

    More in the wiki →

  6. The strength of your Wi-Fi signal. Under it, a status line reads CONNECTED (with the link speed in Mbps), NO NETWORK, MOBILE DATA or DISABLED. The ON light is green on Wi-Fi and yellow on mobile data.

    More in the wiki →

  7. How full your battery is. The LOW light comes on at the low battery level, and the green bolt lights while the phone charges. The small dial shows the battery's temperature, from low (C) to high (H).

    More in the wiki →

Your readings stay on your phone

Nothing the gauges read is sent anywhere. Your location is used only for the speed in GPS mode, and only while the wallpaper is on screen. Future's music, calls and messages are read on the phone and stay there.
