Speedometer Live Wallpaper privacy policy

Privacy policy

Last updated 27.10.2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Speedometer Live Wallpaper (the "app") on Android. The app is made by VGraphics ("we", "us").

The short version

  • Account optionalNothing needs a VHub sign-in. Without one, we never ask for your name, email or other contact details.
  • Readings stay on your phoneBattery, CPU, memory, signal, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and your step count are used to draw the wallpaper and never sent anywhere.
  • Location only for speed and widgetsOnly while on screen and never stored. Only the Weather widget sends a rough location (about 10 km) for its forecast.
  • Music & calls stay on your phoneFuture design only, with your permission: never stored or sent.
  • Ads on menus onlyA small banner on the app's menu screens, and a short video only when you ask for a 24-hour style trial. Never on the wallpaper.
  • Crash reports & analyticsFirebase, without your name, location or readings.

Readings from your phone

To move its needles and lights, the wallpaper reads your battery level, temperature and charging state, the power going into or out of the battery, how fast your CPU runs (its clock speed), your memory use, your mobile signal strength, your Wi-Fi signal strength, state and link speed, whether Bluetooth is on, and the date and time. The app doesn't read your Wi-Fi network's name, your contacts, your files or your phone number. The readings stay on your phone, are used only to draw the wallpaper, and aren't saved.

Location (GPS mode only)

The big gauge can show your speed using GPS. This is off until you choose GPS mode in Settings. The app then asks for location permission "while using the app", which Android also grants while the wallpaper is on your screen. The app never asks for location in the background. Your position is used on your phone only to work out your speed; it is not stored, not sent to us and not shared with anyone, including our advertising partner. You can switch GPS mode off, or take the permission away in your phone's settings, at any time. Some widgets use your location too; see Widgets you add.

Widgets you add

A style's layout can hold widgets that read more of your phone. Each one is read only while a layout with it is on your screen, and only once you allow what it needs. Remove the widget, or take the permission away in your phone's settings, at any time.

  • Trip, average and top speed, drive time, 0–100, odometer, heading, altitude and climb rate use GPS location, as GPS mode does. The odometer and your best 0–100 time are saved on your phone.
  • Sun uses your rough location to work out sunrise and sunset on your phone.
  • Weather sends your rough location, rounded to about 10 km (0.1°), to the forecast service of the Norwegian Meteorological Institute, MET Norway (api.met.no), at most every 30 minutes, and keeps the last forecast on your phone. MET Norway receives nothing else about you beyond what any internet request carries, such as your IP address; see its terms of service.
  • G-force, tilt, compass and air pressure use your phone's motion, compass and pressure sensors. The tilt's level, if you set one, is saved on your phone.
  • Steps uses Android's step counter, with your "Physical activity" permission. Today's count is saved on your phone and never leaves it: not to us, not to VHub, not to anyone.
  • Next event reads the title and time of your next calendar event, with your "Calendar" permission. Next alarm reads only the time of your next alarm.
  • Battery time, data speed, storage and volume read your phone's own counters.
  • Music buttons send back, play, pause and next to the app that's playing, through the notification access described below.

Apart from the Weather widget's rough location, none of this leaves your phone. It is not sent to us and not shared with anyone, including our analytics and advertising partners.

Music, calls and messages (Future design)

The Future design can show the song that's playing, with its cover and the volume, and how many calls you missed and messages you haven't read. For this, Android has to give the app notification access, which you turn on yourself in Android's settings; the app offers it when you pick Future, or under Settings. Android warns that an app with this access could read all your notifications. Speedometer Live Wallpaper only reads the title, artist and cover of the song that's playing, the volume, and the number of missed calls and unread messages. It never reads your call log, your text messages, your contacts or what your messages say.

This information is used on your phone only, to draw the wallpaper. It is not stored, not sent to us and not shared with anyone, including our analytics and advertising partners. You can turn notification access off at any time in your phone's settings; Future then shows the gauges without music, calls or messages.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your settings (gauge mode, units, which date, time and lights to show), which designs you own, and which design you're trying for 24 hours and when that trial began, and what your widgets keep: the odometer, your best 0–100 time, today's steps, the tilt's level and the last weather forecast. This stays in the app's storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Your VHub account (optional)

You can sign in to VHub, the one account for all VGraphics apps and games, with your Google account. Nothing in the app needs it. When you sign in, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us, and VHub gives you an account id. VHub keeps your account id, your email address, the VHub name and picture you choose, your level, XP and badges, and Speedometer's counts on your profile: how many days the wallpaper was on, how many styles you have, and how many layouts you made.

Signed in, the app also keeps a small record for you: the free style you picked as your sign-in gift, your free month of layouts, and a copy of your settings and your own layouts, so a new phone gets them back. The record and your VHub account are kept with Google Firebase (VHub's database is in the European Union) until you delete your account. Your phone's readings, your location, and your music, calls and messages never go to VHub.

You can sign out at any time in Settings → Account. To delete your VHub account and everything VGraphics apps keep for it, choose Delete account there, or use the delete page on vgraphics.gr/hub/delete. Purchases stay with your Google Play account. See also VHub's privacy policy.

The layout market

The app has a market of layouts that players made. Anyone can browse it and use a layout, without an account. Publishing a layout, rating one and reporting one need a VHub sign-in. A published layout is public: its name, the layout itself (which widgets go where), the screen shapes and the style it was made for, its share code, your VHub account id, and how many players used it and their star ratings can be seen by anyone in the app, with your VHub name and picture. You can unpublish your layouts at any time.

When you use, rate or report a layout while signed in, we keep a record with your VHub account id (that you used it, your stars, or your report's reason and message), so that each player counts once. Reports are read only by us, to keep the market clean: we may hide or remove a layout, and stop an account from publishing. The creators you hide and the layouts you report are kept on your phone, and in your settings copy when you're signed in.

The market is kept with Google Firebase, in the European Union. Deleting your VHub account deletes your layouts, your uses and your ratings; the totals they added to other players' layouts stay, without your id. Reports are kept for up to 90 days for moderation, then deleted.

Analytics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. Analytics tells us things like which screens and settings are used, when the wallpaper is first set, and on which days it's on your screen, so we can improve the app. Crashlytics sends a report when the app crashes, so we can fix it. They collect an app instance identifier, your device model, operating system and language, your approximate country, and in-app events or crash details. They don't receive your location or your phone's readings. We use this only to improve the app, never to identify you or to show you ads. Your ad choices (below) also tell Firebase whether these events may be used to measure our own ads for the app on Google (Google's consent mode); where you haven't answered yet in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, they count as not allowed. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Advertising

To keep the app free, its menu screens show a small banner ad delivered by Google AdMob; with Speedometer Plus they show none. The live wallpaper on your home screen never shows ads. See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Some designs can be tried for 24 hours by watching a short video ad, also delivered by Google AdMob. A video plays only when you tap "Try for 24 h" and starts the trial only if you watch it to the end; closing it early changes nothing. When the 24 hours are up, the wallpaper goes back to your own design and the app tells you the next time you open it. The wallpaper never shows a video, a banner or a reminder. With Speedometer Plus there are no videos to watch: every design is already yours.

AdMob may automatically collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (and the rough location it reveals), device model, operating system, language, and how you interact with ads. It uses it to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure results, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, to personalize ads. We don't receive this information about you personally.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent before your data is used for personalized ads, using Google's consent tool. If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for ads. You can change these choices at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy → Ad choices. You can also reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in your device's privacy or ads settings.

In-app purchases

Extra wallpaper designs can be bought in the app. The payment is handled entirely by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy. We never see your payment details. The app only receives a confirmation that the purchase was made, so it can unlock the design.

Speedometer Plus is a subscription (monthly or yearly) that unlocks every design and turns off ads while it runs. Google Play bills it and renews it until you cancel, which you can do at any time in Google Play. When you're signed in to VHub, the purchase carries a coded form of your VHub account id, and the app sends the purchase's receipt to VHub. VHub checks it with Google Play and keeps only its state (the plan, whether it's active, and when the paid time ends), so the subscription also works on your other devices signed in to the same account. VHub never sees your payment details. Deleting your VHub account deletes this record; the subscription itself stays with your Google Play account until you cancel it there.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Unless you sign in to VHub (above), we don't hold your name or contact details. We never hold your location, and analytics data isn't tied to any of them. If you have a question about your data or would like anything deleted, email us and we'll help as far as we can. For data collected by our advertising partner, use the links above or your device's ad settings.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling information differently, we will update this page and the date at the top.
