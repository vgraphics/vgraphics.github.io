The look

About Carbon

Carbon is a race car's dash on fine twill carbon fibre: shift lights across the top, the reading huge on a race display with a bar under it, and bar meters for the rest, in white italic numbers with a red line.

  • €2.39, onceBuy Carbon once on Google Play and it's yours. On a new phone with the same Google account, it comes back by itself.
  • One dial, three jobsThe big gauge shows power, CPU clock or GPS speed. Tap it on your home screen to switch, as in every style.
  • Make it yoursCustomize sets Carbon's colours and background, and its units, clock and warning lights.
  • Layouts, and your ownMost styles come in several layouts. You can also drag and resize the gauges and save your own.
