The look

About Rally

Rally is a rally car's instruments: a big white dial in a black knurled ring over a honeycomb grille, a rally computer's reflective LCD with black segments, and a strip of small dials.

  • €2.39, onceBuy Rally once on Google Play and it's yours. On a new phone with the same Google account, it comes back by itself.
  • One dial, three jobsThe big gauge shows power, CPU clock or GPS speed. Tap it on your home screen to switch, as in every style.
  • Make it yoursCustomize sets Rally's colours and background, and its units, clock and warning lights.
  • Layouts, and your ownMost styles come in several layouts. You can also drag and resize the gauges and save your own.
