## What we hold ourselves to

- **The game comes first.** Ads never cover the controls, and the space for a banner is kept from the moment a level
  appears, so nothing jumps when one loads. In the puzzle games a video plays at most once every six levels, at a
  level's end, never in the middle of one.
- **Fair boards.** Every level is the same for everyone, so a leaderboard compares like with like, and a run that used
  a continue or a powerup is kept off it.
- **Nothing you have to sign up for.** Signing in is optional everywhere, and every game plays fully without it.
- **Questions get answers.** Each released game and app has a ticket desk under Help & ideas in its Settings, and
  every ticket and email is read by the person who built the thing it's about.
- **Our own pictures.** Every picture on this site appears in one place only, and each is ours, made for the page it's
  on. ⟦George, optional:⟧ The art is made with Recraft and the music with Suno, on paid plans that give us the
  commercial rights, then picked and edited by hand; sound effects come only from libraries licensed for commercial
  use, each one with its source on record.
