VHub

Delete your VHub account

VHub is the one player account of VGraphics (VGRAPHICS SINGLE MEMBER P.C.) for its games and apps: Exitwise. Deleting it removes your VHub data from all of them.

Three ways to delete it

  1. In a game or app: open Settings and choose "Delete VHub account". "Delete my data" for one game deletes only that game's part, with the XP and achievements earned in it.
  2. On this page: signed in with Google, or with the guest account a VGraphics web game made in this browser, type DELETE and press "Delete my account". This needs JavaScript.
  3. By email: if you can't sign in here (a guest account on a lost device, a Google Play Games account, a CrazyGames account), email gvergidis90@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my VHub account" and your VHub name or player id (Settings shows it). We confirm by email when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: your name (it becomes free for others), your picture, your title and badges.
  • Your XP, level, stats and achievements in every game and app, and your places on the leaderboards.
  • Your friends (you leave their lists too), friend requests, invites and inbox.
  • Your devices and their notification tokens, your online status, your match history, and the links to your Google, Apple or CrazyGames sign-in.
  • What each game keeps about your account. Most of it goes at once; every game confirms within 30 days.
  • Last, the account itself.

What is kept

  • Moderation records: if pictures you uploaded were removed after reports, the strikes stay under a one-way code made from your Google account id, so a blocked uploader stays blocked. They hold no name or picture.
  • Report counts about names and pictures, without the names of the players who reported.
