VHub

One player account for every VGraphics game and app: your name and picture, a level and XP, achievements, and friends you can invite into a room.

What one account gives you

  • One name and picture in every game and app, chosen once.
  • A level and XP from everything you play, wherever you play it.
  • 38 achievements across VHub and its games and apps, on one profile.
  • Friends you add by name or friend code, and invite into a room to play together.
  • Leaderboards: every player by XP, you and your friends by level, and each game's own boards.
